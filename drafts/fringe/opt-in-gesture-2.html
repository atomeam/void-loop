tilt to read · draft · not published

A gesture you turn on, see, and turn off. Off by default. When you tap "turn on", the page asks the browser for motion access (your phone asks you too) and then reads only the forward/back tilt, shown below as a number. Tilt away to scroll down, toward you to scroll up; level stops it. Nothing is stored or sent; "turn off" stops the reading at once. Arrow keys do the same without any sensor.
