A word flashes for a moment; type it as fast as you can and press Enter. Speed brings typos, and this page sorts each one. In a classic study of misspellings, Damerau (1964) found that about 80% were a single slip: one letter added, one left out, one swapped for another, or two neighbours switched. That is why a search box that forgives one slip finds most of what people meant, and why an assistant that does not misses them. This page counts how many of your own slips are single ones, and of which kind. Everything stays in this browser.
