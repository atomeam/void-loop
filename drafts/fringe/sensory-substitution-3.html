find it by ear · draft · not published

Wear headphones. A point is hidden in the square. You only hear it: higher in the square is a higher note, and further right is louder in your right ear. Listen, then tap where you think it is. This is the same idea as devices that let blind people "see" through sound (The vOICe, 1992) or touch (Bach-y-Rita, 1969): people get better with practice. This page keeps your miss distance trial by trial, so you can see whether you improve. Everything stays in this browser.
