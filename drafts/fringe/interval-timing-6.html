full time, empty time · draft · not published

Each round plays two stretches of time, one after the other. One is filled with a steady tone; the other is empty, marked only by a click at its start and a click at its end. Say which lasted longer. On most rounds they are exactly equal. Many people still hear the filled one as longer: the filled-duration illusion (Thomas and Brown, 1974; Wearden and others, 2007). This page counts how often you pick the filled one when the two are equal, against the 50% you would get by guessing. Use sound. Everything stays in this browser.
