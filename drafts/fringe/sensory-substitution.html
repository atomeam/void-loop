cross-sense board · draft · not published

Three glyphs: bar is 1, gap is 0, stack is 10. Read the row, type its sum. The answer is right or wrong, nothing in between: this is arithmetic agreement, not a measure of how well you see or hear. If you turn the tone on, the same row also plays as sound on this device (bar a 440 Hz sine, gap silence, stack a 220 Hz square, 200 ms each). No camera, no microphone, nothing sent anywhere. It does not restore or replace a sense.
