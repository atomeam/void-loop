unfinished business · draft · not published

Ten scrambled words, one at a time. Unscramble each one and press Enter. Half of them are taken away after 15 seconds whether or not you are done, and you never see their answer. After a short pause, write down every puzzle you can remember: the word or its scrambled letters both count. In 1927 Bluma Zeigarnik reported that people remember interrupted tasks better than finished ones. Later attempts to repeat this found the effect small and unreliable (a 2023 review of the studies found no clear memory advantage). So this page tests the idea on you rather than assuming it: it compares how many interrupted and finished puzzles you recall. Everything stays in this browser.
