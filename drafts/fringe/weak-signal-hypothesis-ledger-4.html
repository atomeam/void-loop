how surprising is it · draft · not published

Something odd happened: a dream that came true, a song on the radio just as you thought of it, a friend calling as you picked up the phone. It feels like one in a million. But how many chances were there for something like it to happen? Rare things on one try become likely when the tries are many (Diaconis and Mosteller, 1989, call this the law of truly large numbers). Put in your own guesses below. This does not say whether anything more was going on; it says how often chance alone would hand you the same story. Nothing leaves the page.
