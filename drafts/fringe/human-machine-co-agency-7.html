watch the autopilot · draft · not published

Three gauges sometimes drift out of range (they turn red). An autopilot resets them for you, but not every time. When it misses one, tap that gauge (or press 1, 2 or 3) before it times out. Each block runs about a minute. In some blocks the autopilot is equally good all the way through. In others it switches between better and worse every few seconds. Parasuraman, Molloy and Singh (1993) found people caught far fewer of the automation’s misses when it was steadily reliable than when its reliability changed. They called this automation-induced complacency. This page counts the misses you catch in each kind of block. Everything stays in this browser.
