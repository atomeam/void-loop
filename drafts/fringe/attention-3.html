the blink · draft · not published

Letters flash in one spot, about ten a second. Two of them are digits. Type both digits when the stream ends. Most people miss the second digit when it comes a short moment (roughly 0.2 to 0.5 seconds) after the first, but catch it when it comes right after or later. Psychologists call this the "attentional blink" (Raymond, Shapiro and Arnell, 1992), and it has been repeated in many labs. This page keeps your own results by gap, so you can see your own blink.
