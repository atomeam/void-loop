hold it as long · draft · not published

Each trial plays a tone of some length between 0.4 and 1.4 seconds. Then hold the big button (or the space bar while it has focus) for as long as the tone lasted. Without a clock, people pull every length toward the middle of what they have been hearing: short ones get held too long, long ones too short (Vierordt's law; Jazayeri and Shadlen, 2010). The spread of errors also grows with the length, roughly in proportion (the "scalar property" of timing; Gibbon, 1977). This page measures both on your own trials. Use headphones or a speaker; everything stays in this browser.
