how sure were you · draft · not published

A hunch ledger that scores you. Write a hunch and how sure you are (0–100%). Later, mark whether it happened. The page shows your Brier score (0 is perfect; always saying 50% scores 0.25) and whether your "70%" hunches come true about 70% of the time. A strong feeling is not evidence; this only measures how well your confidence tracks what happens. Everything stays in this browser.
