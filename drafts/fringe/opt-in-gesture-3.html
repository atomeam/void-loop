Drawing gestures, off until you turn them on. Draw one of four shapes in the box: a circle, a check mark, a cross, or a straight line. Each shape does the same thing as one of the buttons below, and the buttons always work. Gestures are hard to discover and easy to misread, so the page says which shape it saw and keeps your own hit rate. Strokes are read in this browser and never sent or stored; only the counts are kept.
