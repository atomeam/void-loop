count the flashes · draft · not published

Press "flash", watch the box, and say how many times the dot flashed: once or twice. Each trial also plays one or two short beeps, and the beeps are not a clue. Shams, Kamitani and Shimojo (2000) found that one flash with two beeps is often seen as two: hearing changes what you see. The page counts how often a single flash reads as two when two beeps play, against when one beep plays, and asks whether the gap is more than chance. Use sound, look at the dot, and keep your eyes still. A hypothesis about your own senses, nothing more; nothing leaves this browser.
