Tap along with a steady beat: one you hear (a click), one you see (a flashing dot), or both at once. People keep time far more steadily with sound than with a flash, and when both come together the sound mostly decides (Repp and Penel, 2002; Repp’s 2005 review of tapping studies). That is the question behind giving one sense’s job to another: timing may travel well into hearing and badly into sight. This page measures your own spread, how far your taps wander around the beat, in each mode. Tap the pad, or press Space with it focused. Everything stays in this browser.
