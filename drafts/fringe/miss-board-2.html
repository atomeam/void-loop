A miss board before the miss: type an ask and see, in Void's own order, which abilities claim it. The first one that claims it answers; if none do, the ask falls to Void's built-in answers (sums, today, the clock) or to the model, and if those miss too it lands on the board. Each ability also lists asks it must not take; if your ask is one of them, that shows too. Everything runs here, in this page; nothing is sent.
