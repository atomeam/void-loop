the wrong lead · draft · not published

Each puzzle is three words with one word that goes with all of them (cottage, swiss, cake: cheese). Some come with a misleading hint beside each word, the way Smith and Blankenship (1991) planted wrong leads. You get 20 seconds. Any you miss come back for a second try, either straight away or after a one-minute break. One study found a break helped only the people who had been misled, as if the break works by letting the wrong lead fade (Vul and Pashler, 2007). This page keeps your own count of second-try solves in all four cases, so you can see whether a break helps you, and when. Everything stays in this browser.
