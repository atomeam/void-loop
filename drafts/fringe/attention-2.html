how awake am I · draft · not published

A two-minute reaction test. A counter starts at random moments; tap the box (or press space) as soon as you see it. Sleep labs use a longer version of this to measure tiredness: the slow taps ("lapses", over half a second) go up when you are short on sleep. This page keeps your own results by time of day, so you can see when you are sharpest. It is a rough home version, not a medical test. Everything stays in this browser.
