do they know · draft · not published

Someone is asked a question and answers. You see only how the answer arrives: how long they took, and whether an "um" came first. Then say how sure you think they are, 1 (guessing) to 5 (certain). The answers are made up and none is right or wrong. In studies, listeners judge a speaker who answers late or after "um" as knowing less (Brennan and Williams 1995; Clark and Fox Tree 2002 on what "uh" and "um" signal). This page measures how much of your own judgement rides on the pause and how much on the "um". Whether those two weights are steady for one person, and differ from person to person, is an open question this page asks. Everything stays in this browser.
