Dots flash for a moment. Type how many you think there were, then choose whose estimate counts this round: yours, or a helper's that you see only after choosing. The helper is unbiased and usually close, but it misses sometimes. Studies find people drop a helper after seeing it err, even when it beats them on average (Dietvorst, Simmons and Massey, 2015), though others find people prefer algorithmic advice before they see it fail (Logg, Minson and Moore, 2019). This page measures both: whose estimates were closer, and how much more often you leave the helper after it loses a round than after you lose one. Everything stays in this browser.
