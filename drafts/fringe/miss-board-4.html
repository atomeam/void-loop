what it hasn't heard yet · draft · not published

A board of unanswered asks keeps growing, but how much of what people will ask has it already seen? Good and Turing's answer (Good, 1953): the chance the next ask is a kind never seen before is about the share of kinds seen exactly once so far. Chao (1984) adds an estimate of how many kinds there are in all, seen or not. Type short labels for the kinds of asks you notice (your own words; nothing here is a real ask from anyone), or let the page draw a made-up stream, and watch both numbers settle. A hypothesis about how much is left to learn, nothing more. Everything stays in this browser.
