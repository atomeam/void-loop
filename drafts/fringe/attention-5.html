the blink · draft · not published

Letters flash in one place, about ten a second. Two digits hide among them. Afterwards, type both digits. When the second digit comes 200 to 500 ms after the first, many people miss it, though they catch it when it comes right after the first or much later. This is the attentional blink (Raymond, Shapiro and Arnell, 1992; reviewed by Dux and Marois, 2009). The page tracks how often you catch the second digit at each gap, counting only rounds where you got the first. Everything stays in this browser. Fast flashing: skip this if flicker bothers you.
