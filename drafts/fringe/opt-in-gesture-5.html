your double tap · draft · not published

A double tap is two taps close enough together that the device counts them as one gesture. That “close enough” is a fixed window someone else chose: Windows uses 500 ms unless you change it, and phone browsers once waited 300 ms after every tap to see whether a second one was coming. Your own hands may be faster or slower than either. Pick a mode, then tap the pad (or press Space or Enter with it focused): double taps the way you naturally would, and pairs of deliberately separate taps. The page finds the window that best tells your two kinds apart, and how many of yours each fixed window would get wrong. Everything stays in this browser.
