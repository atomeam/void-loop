the streak fades · draft · not published

A thousand people take the same test twice. Each score is part real skill and part luck on the day. Pick out the top tenth from the first try and look at their second try: they come back toward the middle, though nobody changed. That is regression to the mean, first described by Galton (1886) and still a common trap when judging a treatment, a coach or a hunch by its best moments (Barnett, van der Pols and Dobson, 2005). Slide how much of the score is skill and watch how far the top group falls back.
