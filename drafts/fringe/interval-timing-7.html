pulled to the middle · draft · not published

The lamp lights for a while, somewhere between half a second and a second and a half. Then press and hold the pad (or Space) for as long as you think it was lit. Over many tries most people make short times too long and long times too short, as if every guess is pulled toward the middle of the times they have seen. Vierordt described this in 1868, and Jazayeri and Shadlen (2010) showed the pull grows when timing is less certain, the way a careful guesser would lean on what is usual. This page fits a line through your own tries: a slope of 1 means no pull, below 1 means pulled to the middle, and the crossing point is the time you get exactly right. Everything stays in this browser.
