words that lie · draft · not published

A colour word appears in coloured ink. Answer the colour of the ink, not the word: press R, G, B or Y, or tap the buttons. When the word and the ink disagree (the word RED in blue ink) most people are slower; Stroop described this in 1935 and it is one of the most repeated findings in psychology (MacLeod’s 1991 review). How much slower is your own cost of shutting out the word. This page keeps it per sitting with the hour of day, so over time you can see whether your cost is higher when you are tired or at a certain time. Everything stays in this browser.
