odd light log · draft · not published

Write down something odd you saw in the sky: when, roughly where you were, and how it looked and moved. The page works out where the sun was at that moment, then checks the usual explanations first: satellites and Starlink "trains" (lit by the sun while your sky is dark, so they show up in the hours around dusk and dawn), bright planets, aircraft, and balloons or lanterns. Official reviews have found most sightings that could be resolved were things like these. What is left is marked "unexplained after these checks", which means only that: it does not say what it was. Everything stays in this browser.
