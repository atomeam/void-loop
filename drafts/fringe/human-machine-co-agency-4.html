Each trial: something presses the button, and a moment later a beep sounds. Then you guess how long the gap was. On "you" trials you press it yourself, whenever you like. On "machine" trials you wait and the page presses it for you, at a moment you don't choose. In lab studies the gap feels shorter when people cause it themselves: "intentional binding" (Haggard, Clark and Kalogeras, 2002), often used as a measure of the felt sense of being the one who did it. This page compares your own guesses for the two kinds. Use headphones; everything stays in this browser.
