the odd one lasts longer · draft · not published

Each round shows five shapes, one after another. The first four are the same and each stays up for half a second. The fifth is shown for a little less or a little more, and you say whether it seemed shorter or longer than the others: "shorter" (or F) or "longer" (or J). In half the rounds the fifth is a new shape, in the other half the same one again. Studies find that a surprising item seems to last longer than a repeated one of the same length (the "oddball effect"; Tse and others, 2004; Pariyadath and Eagleman, 2007). This page finds, for each kind, the length at which you say "longer" half the time, and shows the difference in milliseconds. Screens show things in whole frames, so lengths are a few milliseconds off; the page records what was really shown. Everything stays in this browser.
