tap with the beat · draft · not published

A flash (and, if you turn it on, a click) beats at a steady tempo. Tap the pad, or the space bar, on each beat. People tend to tap a little before the beat; this shows your own average lead or lag and how steady you were, after 16 taps. It is a number about this round, not a judgement. Nothing leaves the device.
