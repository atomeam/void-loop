A dot appears high or low while a tone plays, high or low. Press ↑ or ↓ (or tap the buttons) for where the dot is, as fast as you can; ignore the tone. Many people answer faster when the tone matches the dot (a high tone with a high dot) than when it doesn't, a link between pitch and height found across many studies (Evans and Treisman, 2010; reviewed by Spence, 2011). This page measures that gap for you. Use sound. Everything stays in this browser.
