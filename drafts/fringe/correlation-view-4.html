You will read 39 short acts, each done by someone from group A or group B. Then you guess how many of each group's acts were unkind. In the classic study (Hamilton and Gifford, 1976), both groups had the same share of unkind acts, but people linked the smaller group with them: two rare things seen together stand out and get remembered as a pattern. A review of later studies found the same pull (Mullen and Johnson, 1990). This page tests whether it happens to you. Everything stays in this browser.
