come back to it · draft · not published

Each puzzle is three words that all go with one more word: cottage, swiss, cake → cheese. When you are stuck, press "pass". Half of the puzzles you pass come back right after the next one; the other half come back only after a break of at least five minutes. Studies find that stepping away from a problem helps a little, more for creative problems like these (the "incubation" effect; Sio and Ormerod, 2009, a review of 117 studies). This page compares the two halves on your own puzzles, so you can see whether a break helps you. The puzzles are the kind used in the Remote Associates Test (Mednick, 1962). Everything stays in this browser.
