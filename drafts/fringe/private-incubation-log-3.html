what fills the break · draft · not published

An idea test in three parts. First, list unusual uses for an everyday object for 2 minutes. Then take a 4-minute break, and the page picks what fills it: an easy task (tap a dot when it turns green), a hard task (subtract 7s in your head), or just resting. Last, the same object comes back for 2 more minutes. In one study (Baird and others, 2012) people who did an easy task in the break came up with more new ideas afterwards than people who did a hard task or rested. This page counts your own new ideas after each kind of break, so you can see whether that holds for you. Everything stays in this browser.
