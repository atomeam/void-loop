how big is yes · draft · not published

Tap the grey circle, then the blue one as fast as you can. The blue target changes size and distance each time. Fitts's law (Fitts, 1954) says the time this takes grows with how hard the jump is: the distance divided by the target's width, on a log scale (the "index of difficulty", in bits). That is why a confirm button for something that matters should be big and close, and a destructive one small or far away. This page fits the line to your own taps and shows how many bits a second you move. Misses are counted too. Everything stays in this browser.
