one odd thing after another · draft · not published

Tap "something odd just happened" whenever something strikes you as strange: a coincidence, a déjà vu, a light you can't place. Over days the page asks one question of your log: do your odd moments come in bursts more than chance would give? If they arrived at random, the number per day would vary about as much as its average (an index of dispersion near 1, Fisher's test). If noticing one makes the next more likely, they bunch (a self-exciting process, Hawkes, 1971), and the index climbs above 1. Bunching would say more about attention than about the world; it is a hypothesis about your own noticing, nothing more. Your log stays in this browser.
