hum the answer · draft · not published

No words: hum for about a second. A hum that rises reads as "yes / go on", one that falls reads as "no / done", a level one as "wait". Rising pitch for a question and falling pitch for a statement are common in speech, but people differ, so this shows the pitch line it heard and the slope it measured, not just the verdict. The microphone is used only while you hold the button, only on this device, and nothing is stored or sent. The three buttons below try it without a microphone.
