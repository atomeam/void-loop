the crowd of arrows · draft · not published

Five arrows appear in a row. Answer only the one in the middle: F (or the left button) when it points left, J when it points right. Half the time its neighbours point the same way, half the time the other way. Neighbours pointing the wrong way slow almost everyone down (Eriksen and Eriksen, 1974); this measures by how much for you, and whether the slowdown shrinks right after a row that fought you, as it often does in the lab. A sitting is 48 rows. Your sittings stay in this browser.
