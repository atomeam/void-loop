ear or eye · draft · not published

Tap along with a steady beat, 16 beats at a time. In some rounds the beat is a beep you hear, in others a light you see. Keeping time is much steadier with sound than with flashes: people tapping to beeps vary by a few tens of milliseconds, to flashes by two or three times as much (Repp, 2005, a review of tapping studies). This is part of why rhythm is hard to pass to the eyes when sound is not there, and why a light needs more than blinking to stand in for a beat. Tap the big pad, or press the space bar. The page compares how steady your own taps are by ear and by eye. Everything stays in this browser.
