Each cloud of dots has a hidden correlation, from 0 (no link) to 1 (a perfect line). Slide to your guess and check it. Studies find people judge these by eye fairly well near 0 and near 1, but tend to guess too low in the middle: a real 0.5 often looks like 0.3 (Cleveland and others, 1982; Rensink and Baldridge, 2010). This page keeps your guesses so you can see whether you do the same. Everything stays in this browser.
