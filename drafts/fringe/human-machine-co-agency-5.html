take the helper's word · draft · not published

A cloud of dots shows for one second. Guess how many there were. Then a helper gives its own guess, and you give your final answer. The helper is made up: its guesses are off by about as much as people's usually are, sometimes high, sometimes low. Studies of advice find that people move only about a quarter or a third of the way toward an adviser, even one as good as they are (Yaniv, 2004), while simply averaging the two guesses is usually more accurate than either one (Soll and Larrick, 2009). This page measures how far you move (0% = moved no distance toward the helper, or away from it; 100% = moved at least as far as the helper's guess) and whether your final answers beat your first ones. Everything stays in this browser.
