A dot appears on the left or the right. You answer only the green dots, with your one key (space, or tap your button) as fast as you can, wherever the dot is; ignore the violet ones. Your button sits on the left. In some sittings a machine partner sits on the right and answers the violet dots itself. People acting beside a partner tend to be slower when their target appears on the partner's side, as if they had taken the partner's task into account (the joint Simon effect, Sebanz, Knoblich and Prinz 2003); alone, with the same single key, the effect mostly disappears. Whether a visible machine partner draws the same cost from you is an open question this page asks: it compares your sittings with and without it. Everything stays in this browser.
