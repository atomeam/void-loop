Each round shows a made-up word and two shapes, one soft and round, one sharp and spiky. Pick the shape the word belongs to; there is no right answer. Most people across many languages give soft-sounding words (like "maluma") to the round shape and sharp-sounding ones (like "takete") to the spiky shape: the bouba–kiki effect (Köhler, 1929; Ćwiek and others, 2022, in 25 languages). This page counts how often you follow it, against the 50% you would get by chance. Everything stays in this browser.
