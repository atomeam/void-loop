Video

Watch open films from Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive, each with its licence and who made it, and Verse’s own films; paste a YouTube, PeerTube, Vimeo or Dailymotion link and it plays in that site’s own player. The player has speed, quality, captions, chapters, keyboard keys and picture-in-picture. Playlists, watch later, history and recommendations stay on your device, and films whose licence allows it can be saved to watch offline. No account, no ads.
