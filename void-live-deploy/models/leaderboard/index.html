What the benchmarks measure

Average
The mean of the six scores below.
IFEval
Instruction following: does the answer obey checkable instructions such as "write in under 100 words" or "use no commas".
BBH
BIG-Bench Hard: 23 hard reasoning tasks from BIG-Bench, such as logic puzzles and date understanding.
MATH Lvl 5
The hardest level (5) of the MATH competition problems.
GPQA
Graduate-level, Google-proof questions in biology, physics and chemistry, written by experts.
MuSR
Multistep soft reasoning: long stories (murder mysteries, object placement, team allocation) to reason through.
MMLU-PRO
MMLU made harder: 10 answer choices instead of 4 and more reasoning questions across 14 subjects.

Scores are percentages, normalised so that random guessing scores 0. A higher score on a test is not the same as being better at what you need: try the models yourself in the arena.

About the data

Scores from the Open LLM Leaderboard by Hugging Face, which evaluated open models with the same harness on the same hardware and was archived in 2025; read live from its public table through the Hub's dataset viewer. Only open models are here: closed models' scores are reported by their makers and cannot be checked the same way. Every model links to its page on the Hub.
