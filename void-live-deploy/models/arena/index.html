How the rating works

Each model starts at 1000. After a vote the winner takes points from the loser, more when it beats a higher-rated model (Elo, K = 32). Two models at 1000: the winner goes to 1016 and the loser to 984. A tie moves them toward each other; "both are bad" counts as a tie.

Your table holds only your own votes, kept on this device. A public arena pools millions of votes from everyone; this one shows what you prefer. Each battle spends two answers from Verse's free daily limit.

Runs on Verse's free share of Cloudflare Workers AI: about ten messages a minute, a daily limit shared by everyone (it resets at midnight UTC), and answers of up to about 800 words. The build here may be quantized, so it can differ a little from the files on the Hub.
