What it actually is

The address is named tsundere. Cambridge Dictionary describes a tsundere as an anime character who seems unkind at first and becomes kinder. Who chose the name for this address, and why, is not on the site, so this page does not guess.

A copy the Wayback Machine saved on 2 September 2018 showed the words “Evangelion Internet Radio Station: Japanese Future Funk, CityPop, & JPop” and “How Dog Became a Beneficiary for TF2”. It was a page of names, not one joke.

On 10 November 2026 the live page was titled “Fripp Don't Interact”. It listed the tracks of King Crimson's In the Court of the Crimson King with a start time for each, and it said the album was “with no sound”. That is the job. The list above is that list.
