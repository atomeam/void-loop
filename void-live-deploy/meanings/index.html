Dictionary

This dictionary is for anyone who wants a plain definition of a word, with the part of speech and an example when one exists.

How to look a word up

Type one word, or a phrase of two or three words, and press Look up. Each sense shows the part of speech, up to three definitions, and one short example when the dictionary has one. If the word is not there, the page says so instead of guessing. The link under the result opens that same entry on the original site.
