        How it works

        Your browser opens the file and Verse writes the new one. Video: the browser plays it once, Verse draws each picture (with the cut, the turn, the text or the picture on top), the browser codes it with its own video encoder (H.264 or VP9) and the sound with its audio encoder, and Verse writes the MP4, MOV, WEBM or MKV file itself. A video takes about as long as it takes to play; a heavy edit on a slow device plays slower so that no picture is lost. 3GP, AVI, FLV and WMV are not written yet: each one says why in its row below. Sound and pictures: WAV, AIFF, FLAC, BMP, TIFF and GIF with Verse's own encoders, OGG and OPUS with the browser's Opus encoder in Verse's own Ogg pages, JPG, PNG and WebP with the browser's, PDF with pdf-lib, and the words of a PDF read with pdf.js. Sounds the browser can play (MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC, the sound of an MP4) are opened by the browser. Encrypted or protected files cannot be read, here or anywhere.
