Tournament standings

Score a Fortnite-style scrim in the browser. Type a result or paste a CSV and the page works out points, standings and the official tiebreakers. Placement pays the highest matching threshold, so a win is worth the win points rather than the win plus the top-five points. Split the lobby into heats, claim drop spots, and share the whole set as a link.

Point system

Results

Pick a match, then tab through each team's place and eliminations. Teams carry over from match to match, and "alpha" or " Alpha " is the same team as "Alpha". Leave a place blank when a team sat a match out.

Place and eliminations for every team in the chosen match

Advanced: type the raw results, one match per line

One match per line: Team:place:eliminations, comma-separated, or separate matches with ;. A line is the whole match. This is the same text the share link carries.

Standings

Standings after every match entered

Tiebreakers, in this order: points, then wins, then eliminations, then average placement. Each match column reads points (place/elims). The gap column says how far a team is behind 1st, or behind the last team that qualifies when a cutoff is set, and what one match needs to pass. With match point on, a team that reaches that many points must then win a match to take the tournament.

Heats

Teams are seeded by standing rank and dealt in snake order, so the top team opens the first heat and the last heat gets the next best.

Drop spots

One per line: Team:Place. Two teams on the same place are flagged contested.

CSV in and out

Paste rows of team, place, eliminations and press Import: the rows become the next match. Export writes the standings, not the raw results.

This is a worked example: three teams, one match, the numbers the seeded ask carries. Change a number or a result and it becomes yours, and it stays on this device.

Everything on this page is worked out on this device from the results you type: no account, no ads, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works offline with the numbers it last had. Placement points go to the highest matching threshold; the tiebreakers are points, wins, eliminations, then average placement.
