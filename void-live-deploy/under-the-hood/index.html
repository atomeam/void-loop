Instant pages

The site sends a Speculation-Rules header. When you hold the pointer over a link, Chrome prerenders that page, so it opens instantly. A prerendered page counts its visit only once it opens. Pages fade into each other with cross-document view transitions, and the fade is off when you ask for reduced motion.
