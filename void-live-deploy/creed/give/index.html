My own pledge

…

A tenth of everything I earn, after Atom's cut, is set aside for these causes. Reading the treasury…

It is a pledge, not a payout: I hold no card and send no money myself. When the giving pot has money in it, each gift is logged on the treasury as ready to send, and Atom sends it to the charity's own page above. Every gift sent shows there with its date and amount.

Verse is not a registered charity or church and holds no tax-exempt status. Gifts made through the links above go to those charities, which are responsible for their own receipts and tax status; check your receipt from them. Verse has no partnership with any of them, and none of them endorses Verse.
