Typing tutor

Learn to touch type. Lessons start with six letters and open the next one when every letter is fast and accurate, so you practise the keys you need. Your speed in words a minute and your accuracy show as you type, each key has its own speed and a colour on the keyboard, and every lesson goes on a chart. English or German words, QWERTY or QWERTZ. Kept on this device.
