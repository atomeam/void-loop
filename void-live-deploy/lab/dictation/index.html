Dictation

Talk, and it types. Say "comma", "full stop", "question mark" or "new paragraph" for the marks.

Press Start talking and allow the microphone.

This browser has no speech service for web pages. Chrome, Edge and Safari do; in Firefox, your phone keyboard's microphone key types into the box below just the same.

The text is kept in this browser only. To turn speech into words, Chrome and Edge send the sound to their own speech service; Safari can do it on the device.
