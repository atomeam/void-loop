A collection of translated AI research reports

Read 13 November 2026. Earliest note on that page: 17 December 2025. Newest note on that page: 26 October 2026.

What it was

A public reading list. The page title, in Chinese, means a collection of Chinese translations of AI research reports. The line under the title says it gathers translations of reports published by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, and others.

When

Who started the page is not named on the home page, and no earlier saved copy was used for this entry. On 13 November 2026 the notes were grouped by year. The newest note was dated 26 October 2026 and was an Anthropic registration statement. Notes still on that same page go back at least to 17 December 2025.

What it did

A visitor could read a translated report. One control switched the page between a light ground and a dark ground. There was no search box, no editor, and no account. The page did not change a number, a file, or a route.

An exclusive-hosting brochure

First saved home page: 5 April 2013. Read again 13 November 2026.

What it was

A one-page brochure for exclusive web hosting. The page called itself a Lianudom company. It used a stock site template: a logo, a headline, a laptop picture, and a row of icons.

When

The Internet Archive's earliest saved copy of the home page is dated 5 April 2013. Later saved copies exist for 2014, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026. Who founded the company, and whether the page has changed hands, is not in the sources read for this entry.

What it did

On 13 November 2026 the headline offered exclusive hosting. Six icons (approval, a comment, wifi, mail, share, and an announcement) each pointed at the same page. A "Chat now" link opened a Messenger thread. There was no price table, no signup form, and no calculator. A reading on 27 October 2026 had already described the page as about seventy words with those unnamed icon links and nothing a visitor could operate.

The arithmetic for bandwidth, workers, and allowed downtime is a separate Verse page, the traffic and uptime calculator. That calculator is not this brochure.
