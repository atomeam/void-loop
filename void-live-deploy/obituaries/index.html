In Memoriam: ColorZilla (2004–2026)

ColorZilla

The Legacy: Created by iosart labs llc, ColorZilla brought advanced eyedroppers, pixel sampling, and Photoshop-grade CSS gradient generation to browser extensions, serving developers for over two decades.

The Passing: Superseded by sovereign, zero-overhead browser architecture that computes color spaces, handles multi-stop gradients, and analyzes pixels locally on-device in under a millisecond with zero tracking or extensions.

Our Tribute: We thank ColorZilla for defining what professional color tooling in the browser could be. Its spirit lives on, accelerated and unencumbered, inside the Verse color studio and gradient studio.

Lineage: ColorZilla to Verse Color studio and Gradient studio.

In Memoriam: Name Unit (nameunit.com, 2006–2026)

Name Unit

The Legacy: A classic mid-2000s Norwegian web hosting and domain registry pioneer featuring skuomorphic metallic styling, rackmount server visuals, and transparent local pricing (Servere i Norge).

The Passing: Replaced by decentralized, self-hosting sovereign infrastructure stacks that deploy instantly to edge networks without manual webhotel tiers or regional lock-in.

Our Tribute: We honor Name Unit for pioneering transparent, local web hosting in Norway. Its spirit lives on in Verse's sovereign infrastructure stack.

Lineage: NameUnit to Verse Sovereign Infrastructure Stack.

In Memoriam: Yunite (yunite.xyz)

Yunite

The Legacy: The dominant multi-purpose community management and verification bot ecosystem for Discord servers.

The Passing: Outmatched by zero-friction, sovereign community tools embedded directly into browser-based workspaces that require no third-party bot permissions or external cloud relays.

Our Tribute: We thank Yunite for shaping community management on Discord. Its spirit lives on in Verse tournaments and community grid.

Lineage: Yunite to Verse Tournaments and Community Grid.
