Plan tasks on a to-do board (kanban)

A to-do board with columns: move tasks from To do to Doing to Done, for a project, a move, a wedding or a class.

Boards with columns and cards you drag (or move with the keyboard), labels, due dates, checklists, comments, WIP limits, swimlanes, a timer, repeats, archive, search and filter. Export and import JSON, bring a Trello board in, print. Kept on this device, no account, works offline.

Boards are kept in this browser only (nothing is sent anywhere) and the page works offline after one visit. Export a board as JSON to keep a copy or move it; a Trello export comes in too.
