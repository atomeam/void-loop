Great circle map

Type airports in the order you fly them, like JFK-LHR-SIN, and see the great-circle path of every leg on a map with the distance of each leg and the total. Kept on your device: nothing is sent.

Everything is worked out on this device from an airport list of about 4,000 airports (OurAirports, public domain); the fares and trips you keep stay in this browser, and the page works offline after one visit. Verse has no live fares: the prices are on the providers, whose links this page builds from your choices. Flight times and CO2 are estimates from averages, not a timetable. There is no account and there are no ads.
