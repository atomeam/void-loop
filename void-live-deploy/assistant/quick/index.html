    What it can and cannot do

    • It answers with a smaller open model on Verse's free share of Cloudflare Workers AI, not a frontier model: it knows less, reasons less deeply and makes more mistakes than the largest assistants. Check anything that matters.
    • There is a daily free limit shared by everyone, and about ten messages a minute each. When it is reached the page says so and keeps your chat.
    • It sees only this conversation's text (the last 16 turns): no web browsing, no images or PDFs read by the model, no memory across chats, no connectors to your accounts.
    • It writes code, and JavaScript runs right here in your browser, sealed off from the page; it does not run other languages or make files for you.
    • Verse builds itself: questions it cannot handle well become work it takes on, so it grows from what people ask.
    • No account: your chats, notes and instructions stay on this device. Each message is sent once to be answered and is not stored by Verse.
