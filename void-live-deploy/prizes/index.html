Keep or send it

This page only does the arithmetic. It never pays out, takes a payment or holds anything; every number is worked out in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. A slice that pays more than it costs on average is rare: when the expected value per spin is below the cost, the house keeps the difference over time.
