How Verse raps
  • Written for your settings each time by Verse's free model (Gemma on Workers AI): two verses of eight bars and a four-bar hook, rhyming in couplets. Kept clean, and never about a real person by name.
  • Each bar is lettered by its end rhyme, so the rhyme scheme shows (the same letter rhymes). The rhymes are found from the words' spelling, so a slant rhyme can go unlettered.
  • The beat is produced for the style in your browser: drums with swing, a sliding 808 for trap, drill and melodic or a round sub bass for boom bap, seventh chords on keys or pads, the music ducking under each kick, then a bus compressor and a limiter. An intro, the verses, an outro. Boom bap sits at about 90 BPM, trap and drill at half time.
  • Perform it and Verse raps in Verse's own voice: each bar is said by a speech model, cut into syllables and put on the beat's grid, the rhyme landing on the snare and doubled left and right. The bar being rapped lights up. You can pick your browser's own voice instead, and Rap it yourself plays only the beat and the lights.
  • Save the beat or the whole song as a stereo WAV, or Make the video: a 1280 x 720 music video with the words lit as they are rapped over a deep space scene that moves with the beat, made and coded in your browser as an MP4 to play or download.
  • Verse listens too. Give it a rap track from your device and it measures the tempo, the swing of the off 16ths, how busy the flow is, the loudness (LUFS), the punch and the balance from sub bass to air, then scores its own take against the track. It changes its tempo, swing and master to get closer, renders the take again with the same voice, and shows the score before and after. Keep this take and Perform, Save the song and the video use it. The track is heard in your browser and never uploaded; only its numbers are kept, in this browser.
  • Share this rap puts the whole rap in the link, so it opens exactly as written. The same settings bring back the same rap for a week; Another take writes a new one. No account, nothing kept but what the link carries.
