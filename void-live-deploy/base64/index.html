Or drop a file here. It is read on this device and never uploaded.

Encoded and decoded on this device as you type, in both directions; nothing is sent anywhere, and the page works offline after one visit. Base64 and Base64URL as RFC 4648 writes them: decoding takes either alphabet, with or without padding, ignores spaces and line breaks, takes a whole data: URL, and names the character that is wrong instead of guessing. Text is UTF-8 unless you pick another character set, so emoji and accents come back exactly. Ask Verse anything
