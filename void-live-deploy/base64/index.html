Encoded and decoded on this device as you type, in both directions on one page: what base64encode.org and its sister site base64decode.org split across two addresses lives in two boxes here. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the page works offline after one visit. Base64 and Base64URL as RFC 4648 writes them: decoding takes either alphabet, with or without padding, ignores spaces and line breaks, takes a whole data: URL, and names the character that is wrong instead of guessing. Text is UTF-8 unless you pick another of the 44 character sets (Unicode, the ISO-8859 family, the Windows code pages, KOI8, Mac, DOS, EBCDIC, and Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, ISO-2022-JP, GBK, GB18030, Big5 and EUC-KR), every one both ways, so emoji and accents come back exactly; a character the set cannot hold is named, never turned into ?. The Asian sets are written from the browser's own decoder, swept once and turned around on this device: no table is downloaded.
