Traffic, queue & uptime calculator

Calculate traffic capacity, queue wait times, server sizing, and allowed downtime for any service or venue.

The same sums answer "how many tills, agents or servers do we need so nobody waits long?" for a shop, a support line or a website. For a web server it goes on to work out sustained bandwidth, monthly transfer, concurrent workers, vCPUs and RAM from your own traffic, the downtime each uptime promise (SLA) allows, and a hardened NGINX or Caddy config with TLS 1.3, security headers and a rate limit. Everything is worked out on this device.

Queue and wait time

How: on average arrivals × time each are being served at once (Little's law); the chance of waiting and the average wait come from the Erlang C formula, which call centres use for the same sum. Arrivals are taken as random, as they are in a shop or on a phone line.

Everything on this page is worked out on this device from the numbers you type: no account, no ads, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works offline with the numbers it last had. Bandwidth is decimal (1,000 KB to the MB); workers come from Little's law, capacity from one core at the worker time you give; the downtime table is 365 days, with a month as a twelfth of that and a week as seven days.
