Rotate letters

Type or paste. Pick how many places to shift, from 1 to 25. Letters move. Everything else stays. Shift by 13 twice and you are back where you started.

The address of this page is the share. With scripts on, it updates as you type.

More text tools: Base64, unit converter, word counter, Versus.

The original site

rot13.com has been a letter shifter since at least a saved copy from 2001. The page does not say who made it, so this one does not guess. The trick is older than the site: a letter shift of 13, used for centuries and still its own undo. The live site is the one Verse went up against. Ours still answers when script is off, and the link keeps the text.
