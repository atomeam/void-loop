This is our motherfucking version

A page should load, say its piece, and get out of the way. This one is dark because the rest of Verse is dark. It asks for no custom font and no picture. The words are the whole product, and they are still here if you turn style and script off.

Fat pages are a choice. A photograph of a button, a font that has to arrive before a sentence does, a script that runs before you can read: that is how a page gets slow. Write the sentence. Link the next thing. Stop.

Weigh some markup

Paste HTML. You get the byte count, and how many pictures, font requests and scripts are in it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

From here: speed and headers, measured, the other Versus tools, word counter. Ask Verse is in the bar.
