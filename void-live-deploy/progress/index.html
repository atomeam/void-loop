What Verse can actually do

Each score is the share of objective checks a real ask passed on a phone-width page: the right skill, a result in 12 seconds, the thing the ask named, and — where a free source has an answer — that source's answer, not a different one. Ambiguous asks have to say there is more than one. A song counts when an audio file plays and the title and artist match iTunes. A holdout set, rotated each week and not published, is scored beside the public one. A new scale is a new baseline. Counts of skills, pull requests and lesson sentences are not a score.
