Cron Schedule Explainer

This page is for anyone who has a cron line and wants it said as a sentence, with the next times it would run.

The original: crontab.guru

A cron line has five fields: minute, hour, day of the month, month, and day of the week. This page reads that line as a sentence and lists the next times it would run, using this device's clock. Named shortcuts such as @daily work too.

    Share this line: /schedules/?line=0%209%20*%20*%201-5

    24 hours in minutes

    Examples

    • */15 * * * *, every 15 minutes.
    • 0 9 * * 1-5, weekdays at 9:00.
    • @daily, once a day at midnight.
    • @reboot, when the computer starts.

    How the symbols work

    A star means every value in that field. A comma lists values, a dash is a range, and a slash is a step. Press a field name to select that part of the line, then press a symbol to put it there. Random picks a common line. Copy puts the line on the clipboard. Show more lists further times, up to 30.
