Cron Schedule Explainer

This page is for anyone who has a cron line and wants it said as a sentence, with the next times it would run.

The original: crontab.guru

Five fields: minute, hour, day of the month, month, day of the week. The page says that line in a sentence and lists the next three times it would run, in this device's clock. @daily and the other named shortcuts work too.
