Social

Communities with a feed sorted hot, new or top, posts and threaded comments, votes, following and an RSS feed for each community. No account: the communities you follow stay on this device.

Posts and comments are public, on Verse's own boards: the same rules, reports, rate limits and bans apply (rules). A vote is kept as one row per post per connection under a keyed hash of the address, never the address. What you follow, save or hide stays in this browser. No account, no ads, no tracking.
