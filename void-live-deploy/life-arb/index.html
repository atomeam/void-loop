Life arbitrage

Private to Atom and Verse. Real gaps between what something costs you, in money and minutes, and what it is worth, found on a schedule and ranked by net value per hour of your time, deadlines first. Every value is one its source states; your minutes are an estimate, shown with its basis. Nothing here buys, signs up or claims anything: each of those waits for your yes.
