Email

Write an email with templates and a spam check, then send it from your own mail in one tap; read the .eml and .mbox files your mail program saves, search them, and see what a message's headers say and where to unsubscribe. Everything runs on this device.

Verse has no mailbox: it does not send or receive email. Sending real mail needs a mail server that other servers trust, and running one well (deliverability, spam and abuse) is a service of its own; Verse opens your draft in the mail you already use instead. Mail files you open are read in this tab and forgotten when it closes; your drafts and signature stay in this browser. No account, no ads.
