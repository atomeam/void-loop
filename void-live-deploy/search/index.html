Ask a question or type words. Verse answers with its sources, shows what Wikipedia and Wikidata have, and opens the same search on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave or Wikipedia. Bangs like !g cats jump straight there.

Verse does not crawl the web: answers come from a language model with Wikipedia as its sources (check them), results from the Wikipedia and Wikidata APIs (CC BY-SA, CC0), and the engine links open the same search on those engines. Bangs and your history run on this device and never leave it. The page opens offline after one visit. No account, no ads, no tracking.
