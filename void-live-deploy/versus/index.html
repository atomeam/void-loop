Versus

Three working tools. Verse is a large site; this is one corner of it.

  • Our motherfucking version. A dark page you can actually read, plus a weigh-up of any markup you paste. The original is motherfuckingwebsite.com.
  • Track times. A timed list you can read, copy, total, and replace with your own. The original is tsundere.pw.
  • Rotate letters. Shift letters by a number you pick. It still works with scripts off. The original is rot13.com.
