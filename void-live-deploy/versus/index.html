Versus

Three working tools. Verse is a large site; this is one corner of it.

  • A readable page. Dark type from Verse, plus a count of the bytes, pictures, fonts and scripts in markup you paste.
  • Track times. A timed list you can read, copy, total, and replace with your own.
  • Rotate letters. Shift letters by a number you pick. It still works with scripts off.
