Chat

Private chats by invite link, end to end encrypted in your browser: groups, pictures, read receipts, and voice and video calls straight between browsers. No account, no phone number.

Messages are sealed in your browser with a key that lives only in the invite link after the #; Verse's relay keeps the sealed messages 7 days (pictures 3) so the others can fetch them, and cannot read them. Calls go straight between browsers through free public STUN servers and may fail behind strict networks (no TURN relay: it costs money). Your chats are kept on this device. No account, no ads.
