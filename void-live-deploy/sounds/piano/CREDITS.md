# Verse's grand piano: where the sound comes from

Every file here is a recording of a real piano. Verse plays them as the keys of its beats (lib/rap-piano.js). tools/piano.py rebuilds them from the originals: mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit, the hammer moved to the first sample, cut at 3 seconds with a half-second fade.

- From the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
- Instrument: "Grand Piano, Steinway B", the sustain-pedal set (Sus), close microphones.
- Files: {note}-soft.wav and {note}-hard.wav for the MIDI notes 36 to 84 every 4 semitones (C2, E2, G#2, C3, E3, G#3, C4, E4, G#4, C5, E5, G#5, C6), from the velocity layers vl2 (soft) and vl4 (hard), round robin 1.
