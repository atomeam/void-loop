How to format chapters Verse
  1. Start a story as a new thread on its fandom board. The subject is the story's title; put the rating, the characters and a one-line summary at the top of the first post.
  2. Post each next chapter as a reply in the same thread, starting with its number and name, like Chapter 2: The Long Way Round.
  3. One post holds up to 4,000 characters and 120 lines. The Writing Room cuts a longer chapter into parts at paragraph breaks for you, so nothing is lost.
  4. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. A line that starts with > shows as a quote, and [spoiler]words[/spoiler] hides words until tapped.
  5. Want later chapters to show they are yours? Post as name#password: the board shows your name and a mark only that password makes (a tripcode). The password is never kept.
Report spam or abuse here Verse

Every post on every board has a Report button. Three reports from different people hide a post until it is looked at. Use it for spam, flooding, personal information about anyone, a story on the wrong board or with the wrong rating, and anything illegal. For a takedown or anything urgent, write to atom@a-to-mind.com.

Writing rules and guidelines Verse
  • The board rules apply here: anything legal, no doxxing, illegal content removed and reported.
  • Adult stories go only on boards marked 18+. Sexual content involving anyone under 18 is never allowed, in any fandom, whatever the canon says.
  • Post your own writing. Credit a co-writer, a beta reader or a prompt you answered.
  • Tag what a reader would want warned about (major character death, violence, self-harm) at the top of the first post.
  • Be kind in replies. Critique the story, never the writer.

    Writing Glossary

    AU
    Alternate universe: the characters you know, put in a different world or a different turn of events.
    Canon
    What the original book, show or game says happened.
    Headcanon
    Something you believe about a character that canon never says.
    OC
    Original character: one you made up and added to someone else's world.
    One-shot
    A complete story in one chapter.
    Drabble
    A very short story, traditionally exactly 100 words.
    WIP
    Work in progress: a story still being written, chapter by chapter.
    Beta reader
    Someone who reads a draft before it is posted and helps fix it.
    Ship
    A pairing of two characters you want together; an OTP is your one true pairing.
    Slow burn
    A relationship that takes a long time to happen.
    Fluff
    Light, warm, happy writing. Angst is its opposite: pain and longing.
    Hurt/comfort
    One character is hurt and another looks after them.
    Crossover
    Characters from two or more stories meet.
    Fix-it
    A story that changes something in canon the writer did not like.
    Prompt
    An idea or line someone offers for others to write from.
    Rating
    Who the story is for. Here a board is either work-safe or 18+, and adult stories go only on 18+ boards.
