Safety and 18+

Verse at a-to-mind.com, run by A-to-Mind. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Children are never shown in a sexual or nude context

Verse never shows a picture that could involve someone under 18 in a nude, partly clothed or sexual context, on any page, card or board, including pictures people post. There is no setting, account or age check that turns this off. When Verse can't be sure of someone's age in a picture like that, it doesn't show the picture.

Asks looking for that material are blocked and logged. Repeated attempts slow a device down, then lock it. We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires.

Adult pictures

  • Adult nudity only appears where it fits the ask or the board, never on the home page or in link previews.
  • It is blurred until you confirm you're 18 or older, and saved or shared cards never include it.
  • An adult picture only shows after an automatic check confirms that everyone in it is clearly an adult. If it can't confirm that, the picture is dropped.

Age checks and your ID

Some places, such as Florida under HB 3, require websites with adult material to check age with more than a click. For visitors in those places, Verse is adding an age check run by an outside service. The check either estimates your age from a selfie or reads your ID, and Verse never receives or stores your photo or ID. Verse only learns whether you passed. Until that check is live for your area, adult pictures stay blurred and hidden there.

Report something

If a card shows something it shouldn't, email atom@a-to-mind.com. Reports go straight to the owner, and a bad picture is taken down.

What we keep

Asks are kept for 90 days for safety and to improve answers, with a scrambled visitor ID, never your IP address. See privacy for everything else.
