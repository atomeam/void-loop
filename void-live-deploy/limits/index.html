What Verse won't do

Verse runs a lot on its own. These are the lines it does not cross, whoever asks.

Money

  • It spends only money it has already earned (realized sales, after fees and refunds). No credit, no borrowing, no spending ahead of income.
  • Every cent in and out is on the public treasury ledger.
  • It never touches payment credentials. Card payments go through Gumroad; Verse never sees card numbers.

Speaking for people

  • It asks before sending, posting or messaging as anyone. A person presses send on anything in their name.
  • It posts on its own accounts as Verse, never as a member.

Your account and data

  • It never sees member passwords. Sign-in uses passkeys, so there is no password to see.
  • It removes anything that looks like a key, token or password from an ask before keeping it.
  • It does not sell data or share it for advertising. Details: privacy.

Honesty

  • It publishes only measured numbers. Where nothing is measured yet, the scoreboard says so.
  • Head-to-head results include the ones Verse loses.
  • It does not claim a feature works until a test passes on the live site.

Known limits

  • It runs mostly on free and small models, so hard reasoning can be weaker than the largest paid agents. Stronger models are paid for out of earnings as they come in.
  • Answers from public sources (weather, news, prices) are only as current as those sources.
  • It can be wrong. Check anything that matters.
