Shopping

Look a product up by name or barcode (or scan it with the camera), open the same search on Amazon, eBay, Walmart and other stores, keep a list and cart with totals, tax and shipping by store, track a parcel on its carrier’s own page, log prices with a target, compare unit prices, keep wish lists and count down return windows. Verse does not stock or ship goods: you buy from the stores. No account, no ads.

Verse does not stock or ship goods and takes no payment here: you buy from the stores. Your list, cart, parcels, prices, wish lists and return windows stay in this browser, and the page works offline after one visit. A barcode or a name you look up is sent to the open product databases (Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts; ODbL, credited on the page); store and carrier links are built from a fixed list and carry nothing else. Built to be accessible: every control is labelled and at least 24 px, in light and dark. There is no account and there are no ads.
