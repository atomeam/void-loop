Body text at 16px: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
Large text, 24px bold

  
Nearest colours that pass

  
Contrast is WCAG's ratio of relative luminance: 4.5:1 for body text (AA), 3:1 for large text (18pt, or 14pt bold) and for buttons, icons and focus rings (1.4.11), 7:1 and 4.5:1 for AAA. The suggestions keep the hue and move the lightness the least.
