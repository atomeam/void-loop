          Events, reminders and invites stay in this browser; an event can carry a location with a map link. Tasks with a due day sit beside events. An invite is a link that carries the event itself, or an email with the .ics file; guests answer yes, maybe or no (RSVP) and see who is coming. Several calendars, each with its own colour and an on/off switch: yours, birthdays, public holidays by country. Share a calendar by a view link or an edit link, no account; subscribe to any calendar by its address (.ics or webcal). Only a shared calendar, a subscription and invite replies go to Verse's server.
