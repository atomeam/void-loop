Text Compare

This page is for anyone who has two copies of a text and wants to see what was added, what was removed, and what stayed.

The original: diffchecker.com

Paste an original and a changed copy. Every line that was removed, added, or left as it was is marked. A space, a blank line, and a change of case all count. The work stays on this device. Up to 3000 lines on a side.

Share this comparison: /text-diff/?original=The%20quick%20brown%20fox

How to use it

The boxes open on two short lines about a fox. One line is the same. One line was removed, and two lines were added. A space and a change of case both count until you set them aside. Paste, or choose a text file for either side. Press Compare if scripts are off. The address still holds both texts.

What this page leaves out

This page compares text, on this device, and does not keep a copy. It does not compare pictures, PDFs, Word files, or folders. It does not save a comparison on a server, and it does not add comments. A change inside a line is shown as the whole line. Setting case or extra spaces aside is a choice on this page. The default still counts both.
