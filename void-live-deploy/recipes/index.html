                A dozen classics are built into the page and need no network. Every other recipe comes from TheMealDB, a free public recipe catalogue; its recipes carry no servings, so 4 is assumed and said. Nutrition per serving is an estimate from the ingredients (kcal, protein, fat, carbs), and so is the cost (typical US supermarket prices, a rough guide). Diets and allergies (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, egg-free, nut-free) and "without" any ingredient filter what you find. A shopping list collects from several recipes; save recipes into collections, with your own rating and notes. Only the words you search go to Verse; your history, list, saved recipes, ratings and ticks stay on this device.
