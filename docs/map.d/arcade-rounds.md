The arcade rounds, each a Verse-brand game you play against Verse on `/arcade/`: the two that play on the cabinet are **Gap Glide** (`skills/gap-glide.js`, `skills/gap-glide-rules.js`, one glider through gates that tighten, with a storm gate) and **Debris Field** (`skills/debris-field.js`, `skills/debris-field-rules.js`, one ship with free movement, debris from every edge, a daily seed and a best score on the device). The other ten are studio rounds with pure rules, a seed and Verse as player two: **Cross the Bus** (`skills/cross-bus.js`, `skills/cross-bus-rules.js`, cross the lanes of moving blocks, a block sends you home), **Null Waves** (`skills/null-waves.js`, `skills/null-waves-rules.js`, a block of plain rectangles steps down and Verse drops a request toward your column), **Packet Maze** (`skills/packet-maze.js`, `skills/packet-maze-rules.js`, collect packets in a walled grid, Verse moves half as often), **Jump Stack** (`skills/jump-stack.js`, `skills/jump-stack-rules.js`, both climb the same ledges), **Stackwell** (`skills/stackwell.js`, `skills/stackwell-rules.js`, two wells of original small pieces), **Push Packets** (`skills/push-packets.js`, `skills/push-packets-rules.js`, slide a packet onto its mark, Verse tugs the pusher the wrong way), **Nine Grid** (`skills/nine-grid.js`, `skills/nine-grid-rules.js`, a 4 by 4 number grid in four boxes, Verse knows the fill), **Glyph Verse** (`skills/glyph-verse.js`, `skills/glyph-verse-rules.js`, six guesses of a five-letter word from Verse's own list), **Three Alike** (`skills/three-alike.js`, `skills/three-alike-rules.js`, swap two neighbours, three of a kind clear) and **Token Stand** (`skills/token-stand.js`, `skills/token-stand-rules.js`, six market days, you set a price). A round link carries the seed and the score (`void-live-deploy/lib/studio-play.js`, `roundLink`), the record goes on the arcade ladder (`void-live-deploy/lib/ladder.js`), and the cabinet maps a screen tap to its row (`void-live-deploy/arcade/host.js`). Tests: `tools/studio-games.test.mjs` and `tools/arcade-rack.test.mjs`.
