`world-probe.mjs` opens a /world/ scene in a real browser and checks that it draws with no errors, every part is there, every behavior and every beyond-reality feature works, and how fast it runs; it saves a screenshot and a short video. `void-live-deploy/world/snail.js` is the garden snail scene (real anatomy and crawling, slow time, see inside, change the weather, look up through glass, tap to feed).
