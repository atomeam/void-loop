The Tower compounds: `tools/tower-compound.mjs` (pure parts in `void-live-deploy/lib/compound.js`) measures every site and target that ever counted again against its live original (`--live` runs `tools/tower-gate.mjs`, `--features` runs `tools/feature-scan.mjs` and diffs the feature names against `domains/features/`). A site that no longer wins every check, or whose original gained a feature Verse lacks, leaves `assimilated` and `passed` in `tower.target.json`, goes into `requeue` (read by `advance` in `tools/tower-target.mjs`, ahead of any new target) and to the front of `background` as must-beat jobs. One row per pass goes to the owner-only `tower.compound.json`, shown on the owner's Versus card (`versus/universe.js`) and in `/progress/`.
