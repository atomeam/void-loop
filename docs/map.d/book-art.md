The book carries no decorative art: `book-art.mjs` (test `book-art.test.mjs`, run by `guard-source.mjs` on every pull request) typesets every page of the Book of Verse and both covers and fails on a multi-colour or filled svg, an emoji or an img. A diagram that carries information may stay as a monochrome hairline schematic (strokes in rgba(180,200,230,0.25), no fills). `lib/tome.js` sets the book as pitch-black glass (`GLASS_CSS`) with monochrome numerals.
