- **blub, the blobfish (`void-live-deploy/blub/index.html`, `blub.js`, `blub.jpg`, 2026-11-19):** a real-time 3D blobfish on /blub/, drawn on the graphics card (WebGL2, a distance field ray marched in one shader): wet translucent skin with light glowing through it, caustics on the sea floor and on the fish, a sagging nose, a downturned mouth, small sunken eyes that follow the pointer, breathing, blinking and a "blub" that opens the mouth and lets bubbles go. A phone gets fewer steps and a smaller picture and the picture scales itself to hold the frame rate; reduced motion draws one still frame; without WebGL2 the page shows a real blobfish photo. It is the messenger of A-to-Mind Verse (the charter's showcase note) and a surface the quality engine scores and improves like any other, against the best real-time characters and mascots.
