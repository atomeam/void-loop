Forge, the routing tournament (#2959, #2960): `evolve-adversary.mjs` turns a skill's example asks into harder near-miss cases, `evolve-mutate.mjs` writes three competing routing mutations as data, `evolve-tournament.mjs` judges them on the same stress suite and bank with hard kill switches, `evolve-merge.mjs` gates a champion before anything is banked, and `evolve-bank.mjs` keeps the immutable regression bank. The skill `automation-handoff` (in the self family) packages the running automations into a card for a client handoff; nothing is sent.
