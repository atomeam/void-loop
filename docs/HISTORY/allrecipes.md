## Key milestones

- 1997: "Allrecipes.com was founded in 1997 after Hunt and Shepherd had trouble finding a cookie recipe on the Internet." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes#History))
- 1999: "In 1999, Allrecipes.com hired Bill Moore, a former Starbucks executive, as its CEO." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes#History))
- 2006: "In 2006, Reader's Digest purchased Allrecipes.com for $66 million." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes#History))
- 2012: "Reader's Digest sold the company to the Meredith Corporation in 2012 for $175 million." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes#History))
