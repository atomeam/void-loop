Created by Condé Nast in 1995, it is headquartered at the One World Trade Center in Manhattan, New York City, where it is part of the publisher's Food Innovation Group that also includes Bon Appétit, with significant overlap in staff between the two companies. ## Key milestones

- 1995: "Epicurious launched on August 18, 1995, as part of CondeNet, a subsidiary of Condé Nast that was created to develop content specifically for the Internet." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Launch_and_early_growth_(1995%E2%80%932004)))
- 1996: "From 1996 to 1997, Epicurious grew to include special initiatives like full Thanksgiving meal recipes lists, and entered into a deal with CNET to provide content for Snap!" ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Launch_and_early_growth_(1995%E2%80%932004)))
- 1998: "The program debuted in 1998 on the Discovery Channel and was hosted by Jonathan Karsh." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Launch_and_early_growth_(1995%E2%80%932004)))
- 1999: "Rochelle Udell left Condé Nast in 1999, and Elizabeth Shepard was named editor-in-chief of Epicurious the following year." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Launch_and_early_growth_(1995%E2%80%932004)))
- 2003: "The site grew to include 16,000 recipes by 2003." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Launch_and_early_growth_(1995%E2%80%932004)))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- "The app launches were part of a larger rebranding strategy aimed at positioning Epicurious as a complete lifestyle brand as well as a destination for recipes." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Tenth_anniversary%2C_smartphone_era_begins_(2005%E2%80%932014)))

## Redesigns

- 2015: "Under Gillin's direction, the Epicurious brand was relaunched on February 3, 2015, with a new logo and new content strategy, focusing on recipe curation and helping home cooks." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Rebrand%2C_relaunch%2C_and_20th_anniversary_(2014%E2%80%93present)))
- "Nilou Motamed was named Editor-in-Chief and began working on a redesign with an eye towards highlighting non-recipe content, like restaurants and travel coverage." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Tenth_anniversary%2C_smartphone_era_begins_(2005%E2%80%932014)))
- "The new design combined the existing recipe curation with new technology and content aimed at enticing and guiding home cooks." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Rebrand%2C_relaunch%2C_and_20th_anniversary_(2014%E2%80%93present)))
- "The mobile apps for Windows and iOS were redesigned as well." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurious#Rebrand%2C_relaunch%2C_and_20th_anniversary_(2014%E2%80%93present)))
