## Notable features

- "Amazon sells many products under its own brand names, including phone chargers, batteries, and diaper wipes. and features hundreds of product lines." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Private-label_products))
- "Amazon owned 34 private-label brands as of 2019." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Private-label_products))
- "These brands account for 0.15% of Amazon's global sales, whereas the average for other large retailers is 18%." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Private-label_products))
- "Amazon allows users to submit reviews of each product." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Product_reviews))
- "Reviewers rate the product from one to five stars and offer commentary." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Product_reviews))
- "Amazon provides a badging option for reviewers that indicates the real name of the reviewer (based on confirmation of a credit card account) or that indicates that the reviewer is one of the top reviewers by popularity." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company)#Product_reviews))
