## Key milestones

- 2006: "Google Translate is a web-based free-to-use translation service developed by Google in April 2006." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2010: "In January 2010, Google introduced an Android app and iOS version in February 2011 to serve as a portable personal interpreter." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2010: "As of February 2010, it was integrated into browsers such as Chrome and was able to pronounce the translated text, automatically recognize words in a picture and spot unfamiliar text and languages." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2014: "In May 2014, Google acquired Word Lens to improve the quality of visual and voice translation." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2016: "In November 2016, Google transitioned its translating method to a neural machine system called Google Neural Machine Translation (GNMT)." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2017: "In 2017, Google Translate was used during a court hearing when court officials at Teesside Magistrates' Court failed to book an interpreter for the Chinese defendant." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2018: "As of 2018, it translates more than 100 billion words a day." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2021: "A petition for Google to add Cree to Google Translate was created in 2021, but it was not one of the languages in development at the time of the Translate Community's closure." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- 2024: "In 2024, a record of 110 languages including Cantonese, Tok Pisin and some regional languages in Russia including Bashkir, Chechen, Ossetian and Crimean Tatar were added." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2015: "In January 2015, the apps gained the ability to propose translations of physical signs in real time using the device's camera, as a result of Google's acquisition of the Word Lens app." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#Mobile_app))
- 2022: "At the end of September 2022, Google Translate was discontinued in mainland China, which Google said was due to "low usage"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#History))
- "The feature was subsequently renamed Instant Camera." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Translate#Mobile_app))
