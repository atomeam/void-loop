## Key milestones

- 1998: "In 1998, despite the economic recession the region was facing at the time, Television Corporation of Singapore (TCS), the predecessor of Mediacorp, during a meeting of its executives, suggested the creation of a news channel (initially rumoured to be a business channel)." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#1998%3A_Background_and_launch))
- 1999: "Under the initially planned format, the channel was set to provide business news mirroring CNN and CNBC, with a tentative launch date of 1 January 1999." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#1998%3A_Background_and_launch))
- 1999: "Channel NewsAsia launched on 1 March 1999, opening with a speech by Minister of Information and the Arts George Yeo at Raffles Place." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#1998%3A_Background_and_launch))
- 1999: "A few months after launch, in July 1999, jurisdiction of CNA was moved from TCS to the newly created MediaCorp News, in line with the renaming of TCS's holding company, Singapore International Media, to Media Corporation of Singapore and the creation of "strategic business units"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#1998%3A_Background_and_launch))
- 2000: "It started to be distributed in other Asian countries and Australia by satellite on 28 September 2000, after a decision taken on 1 March, with the hopes of promoting it as a pan-Asian network." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#1998%3A_Background_and_launch))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2000: "Its first rebrand was in September 2000, made by British firm English & Pockett." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#Visual_identity))
- 2006: "The channel rebranded on 17 July 2006." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#2004_to_2018%3A_Expansion_into_region))
- 2019: "In March 2019, marking the network's 20th anniversary, Mediacorp announced that Channel NewsAsia would officially rebrand as "CNA" full-time, citing a need to abandon a "TV-centric" identity to emphasise its multi-platform operations." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#2019_to_present%3A_Rebranding_and_subsuming_newspaper))

## Redesigns

- 2013: "On 21 January 2013, CNA underwent a major relaunch, introducing a new studio at the Marina Bay Financial Centre, a new Mumbai bureau, an expansion in programming, and the new slogan "Understand Asia"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#2004_to_2018%3A_Expansion_into_region))
- "This would include the relaunch of its news radio station 938Now as CNA938, and the upcoming citizen journalism initiative Tell CNA." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNA_(TV_network)#2019_to_present%3A_Rebranding_and_subsuming_newspaper))
