## Key milestones

- 2016: "The translating system was first developed within Linguee by a team led by Chief Technology Officer Jarosław Kutyłowski in 2016." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2017: "It was launched as DeepL Translator on 28 August 2017 and offered translations between English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Dutch." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2017: "With the release of DeepL in 2017, Linguee's company name was changed to DeepL GmbH, and it is also financed by advertising on its sister site, linguee.com." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2018: "Support for Portuguese and Russian was added on December 5, 2018." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2019: "In July 2019, Jarosław Kutyłowski became the CEO of DeepL GmbH and restructured the company into a Societas Europaea in 2021." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2019: "Translation software for Microsoft Windows and macOS was released in September 2019." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2020: "Support for Chinese (simplified) and Japanese was added on 19 March 2020, which the company claimed to have surpassed the aforementioned competitors as well as Baidu and Youdao." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2021: "Then, 13 more European languages were added in March 2021: Bulgarian, Czech, Danish, Estonian, Finnish, Greek, Hungarian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, and Swedish, bringing the total number of supported languages to 24." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2022: "On 25 May 2022, support for Indonesian and Turkish was added, and support for Ukrainian was added on 14 September 2022." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
- 2023: "In January 2023, the company reached a valuation of 1 billion euro and became the most valued startup company in Cologne." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepL_Translator#History))
