## Key milestones

- 2008: "The funding round came as the company saw a large amount of growth since its launch in 2008." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#History))
- 2010: "In October 2010, Evernote raised $20 million in funding led by Sequoia Capital, DoCoMo Capital, Troika Dialog and Morgenthaler Ventures as the service continued to expand its user base." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#History))
- 2012: "In 2012, Evernote launched Evernote Business, a version of the service for teams that enabled users to share notes and collaborate within an organization." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#History))
- 2012: "Also in 2012, Evernote had launched its China based service under the name, Yinxiang Biji (Chinese: 印象笔记) which in June 2018 transitioned into a company with its own full autonomy." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2011: "Originally developed by Plasq, Skitch was acquired by Evernote on August 18, 2011." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Skitch))
- 2013: "In 2013, Evernote discontinued its direct integration with Twitter, recommending IFTTT and Zapier as alternatives." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Integrations))
- 2016: "Evernote discontinued Skitch for Windows, Windows Touch, iOS, and Android in January 2016." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Skitch))
- 2022: "Evernote was acquired by the Italian technology company Bending Spoons in November 2022." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#History))

## Notable features

- "Evernote clients are available for Android, iOS (iPad, iPhone, and previously, iPod Touch), macOS, Microsoft Windows, and Web." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Supported_platforms))
- "Additionally, portable versions of Evernote are available for flash drives and U3 drives." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Supported_platforms))
- "There are no officially supported native clients for BSD or Linux, but the company provides an API for external Linux clients." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Supported_platforms))
- "On June 11, 2014, Evernote suffered a distributed denial-of-service attack that prevented customers from accessing their information." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Denial-of-service_attacks))
- "The attackers demanded a ransom, which Evernote refused to pay." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Denial-of-service_attacks))
- "A denial-of-service attack on August 8, 2014, resulted in a brief period of downtime for evernote.com; service was quickly restored." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Denial-of-service_attacks))

## Controversies

- "On March 2, 2013, Evernote revealed that hackers gained access to their network and accessed user information, including usernames, email addresses, and hashed passwords." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Security_breach))
- "All users were asked to reset their passwords." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Security_breach))
- "In the wake of this, Evernote accelerated plans to implement an optional two-factor authentication for all users." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Security_breach))
- "In December 2016, Evernote announced its privacy policy would be changing in January 2017, leading to claims the policy allowed employees of the firm to access users' content in some situations." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Privacy_policy))
- "In response to the concerns, Evernote apologized and announced the policy would not be implemented, and that its employees would not have access to users' content unless users opted in." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evernote#Privacy_policy))
