## Controversies

- "Photopea has received positive coverage due to its similarities to Adobe Photoshop in design and workflow, making it an easier program for those trained in Photoshop to use, compared to other free raster image editors such as GIMP." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopea#Reception))
