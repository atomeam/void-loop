## Key milestones

- 2013: "Smallpdf was founded in 2013 by Mathis Büchi, Lino Teuteberg, and Manuel Stofer while they were living abroad in Korea." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpdf.com#History))
- 2018: "In November 2018, it was announced that Smallpdf would integrate with Dropbox as part of the 'Dropbox Extensions', to provide Dropbox users with PDF editing capabilities." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpdf.com#History))
- 2019: "As of 2019, approximately 25 million unique users use Smallpdf every month." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpdf.com#History))
- 2022: "Smallpdf expanded through acquisition in March 2022, buying Zürich-based document-processing software company PDF Tools for US$30 million." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpdf.com#History))
- 2026: "In July 2026, Smallpdf sold PDF Tools to document-software company Apryse." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpdf.com#History))
