## Key milestones

- 2002: "The company was founded in December 2002 by Reid Hoffman and the founding team members from PayPal and Socialnet.com (Allen Blue, Eric Ly, Jean-Luc Vaillant, Lee Hower, Konstantin Guericke, Stephen Beitzel, David Eves, Ian McNish, Yan Pujante, Chris Saccheri)." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2003: "In late 2003, Sequoia Capital led the Series A investment in the company." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2004: "In August 2004, LinkedIn reached 1 million users." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2006: "In March 2006, LinkedIn achieved its first month of profitability." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2007: "In April 2007, LinkedIn reached 10 million users." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2008: "In February 2008, LinkedIn launched a mobile version of the site." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2008: "In June 2008, Sequoia Capital, Greylock Partners, and other venture capital firms purchased a 5% stake in the company for $53 million, giving the company a post-money valuation of approximately $1 billion." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2009: "In November 2009, LinkedIn opened its office in Mumbai and soon thereafter in Sydney, as it started its Asia-Pacific team expansion." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2009: "LinkedIn started its India operations in 2009 and a major part of the first year was dedicated to understanding professionals in India and educating members to leverage LinkedIn for career development." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))
- 2010: "In 2010, LinkedIn opened an International Headquarters in Dublin, Ireland, received a $20 million investment from Tiger Global Management LLC at a valuation of approximately $2 billion, announced its first acquisition, Mspoke, and improved its 1% premium subscription ratio." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Founding_from_2002_to_2011))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2021: "In September 2021, LinkedIn discontinued LinkedIn stories, a feature that was rolled out worldwide in October 2020." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Discontinued_features))
- 2021: "In October 2021, after reports that several academics and reporters had received notifications stating that their profiles were now blocked in China, Microsoft confirmed that LinkedIn would shut down in China and be replaced by InJobs, a China-exclusive app, citing difficulties with the operating environment and increasing compliance requirements." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#International_restrictions))
- 2022: "It was the largest acquisition made by Microsoft, until the acquisition of Activision Blizzard in 2022." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#2011_to_present))
- 2023: "In 2023, LinkedIn also discontinued LinkedIn Audio Events, though LinkedIn Live Video remains available but only through third-party streaming integrations such as StreamYard and Restream." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Discontinued_features))

## Notable features

- "The basic functionality of LinkedIn allows users to create profiles, which for employees typically consist of a curriculum vitae describing their work experience, education and training, skills, and a personal photo." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Basic_functionality))
- "Employers can list jobs and search for potential candidates." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Basic_functionality))
- "Users can find jobs, people and business opportunities recommended by someone in one's contact network." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Basic_functionality))
- "In January 2013, LinkedIn dropped support for LinkedIn Answers and cited a new 'focus on development of new and more engaging ways to share and discuss professional topics across LinkedIn' as the reason for the retirement of the feature." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Discontinued_features))
- "The feature had been launched in 2007 and allowed users to post questions to their network and allowed users to rank answers." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Discontinued_features))
- "In 2014, LinkedIn retired InMaps, a feature which allowed you to visualize your professional network." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Discontinued_features))

## Controversies

- "In 2013, a class action lawsuit entitled Perkins vs." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Perkins_lawsuit))
- "LinkedIn Corp was filed against the company, accusing it of automatically sending invitations to contacts in a member's email address book without permission." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Perkins_lawsuit))
- "The court agreed with LinkedIn that permission had in fact been given for invitations to be sent, but not for the two further reminder emails." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Perkins_lawsuit))
- "LinkedIn settled the lawsuit in 2015 for $13 million (≈$17.2 million in 2025)." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Perkins_lawsuit))
- "The German Stiftung Warentest has criticized that the balance of rights between users and LinkedIn is disproportionate, restricting users' rights excessively while granting the company far-reaching rights." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Privacy_policy))
- "It has also been claimed that LinkedIn does not respond to consumer protection center requests." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn#Privacy_policy))
