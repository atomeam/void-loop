## Key milestones

- 2005: "Cozi was founded in 2005 by Robbie Cape and Jan Miksovsky, two former Microsoft employees who sought to simplify family logistics with technology." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))
- 2006: "The company's first product, Cozi Central, was released on September 25, 2006, and included a family calendar, shopping lists, family messaging and a photo collage screensaver." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))
- 2011: "On June 5, 2011, Cozi set a Guinness World Record for the longest line of ducks in a row." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))
- 2018: "After the Meredith Corporation acquired Time in 2018, Cozi was moved into the Parents Network division." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2014: "Cozi was acquired by Time Inc. in 2014." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))
- 2022: "On May 4, 2022, Cozi was acquired by OurFamilyWizard of Minneapolis, Minnesota, reporting more than 20 million registered users." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozi#History))
