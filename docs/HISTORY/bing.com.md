## Key milestones

- 2009: "The transition from Live Search to Bing was announced by Microsoft CEO Steve Ballmer on May 28, 2009, at the All Things Digital conference in San Diego, California." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing))
- 2009: "The official release followed on June 3, 2009." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing))
- 2008: "These features leveraged semantic technology from Powerset, a company Microsoft acquired in 2008." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing))
- 2016: "Microsoft made significant strides towards open-source technology in 2016, making the BitFunnel search engine indexing algorithm and various components of Bing open source." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2007: "On March 21, 2007, Microsoft announced that it would separate its search developments from the Windows Live services family, rebranding the service as Live Search." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- 2008: "On May 23, 2008, Microsoft discontinued Live Search Books and Live Search Academic and integrated all academic and book search results into regular search." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- 2008: "Windows Live Expo was discontinued on July 31, 2008." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- 2009: "On May 15, 2009, Live Product Upload, a service which allowed merchants to upload products information onto Live Search Products, was discontinued." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- 2009: "The final reorganization came as Live Search QnA was rebranded MSN QnA on February 18, 2009, then discontinued on May 21, 2009." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- 2023: "In February 2023, Microsoft launched Bing Chat (later renamed Microsoft Copilot), an artificial intelligence chatbot experience based on GPT-4, integrated directly into the search engine." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing))
- "As part of this change, Live Search was merged with Microsoft adCenter." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))
- "Live Search Macros, a service for users to create their own custom search engines or use macros created by other users, was also discontinued." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Live_Search))

## Redesigns

- 2012: "In May 2012, Microsoft announced another redesign of its search engine that includes "Sidebar", a social feature that searches users' social networks for information relevant to the search query." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Growth_(2009%E2%80%932023)))
- 2023: "On February 7, 2023, Microsoft began rolling out a major overhaul to Bing, called the new Bing." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#AI_integration_(2023%E2%80%93present)))

## Notable features

- "Bing allows webmasters to manage the web crawling status of their own websites through Bing Webmaster Center." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Webmaster_services))
- "Users may also submit contents to Bing via the Bing Local Listing Center, which allows businesses to add business listings onto Bing Maps and Bing Local." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Webmaster_services))
- "Bing Mobile allows users to conduct search queries on their mobile devices, either via the mobile browser or a downloadable mobile application." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Mobile_services))
- "The Bing Bar, a browser extension toolbar that replaced the MSN Toolbar and Windows Live Toolbar, provides users with links to Bing and MSN content from within their web browser without needing to navigate away from a web page they are already on." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Toolbars))
- "The user can customize the theme and color scheme of the Bing Bar and choose which MSN content buttons to display." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Toolbars))
- "Bing Bar also has the local weather forecast and stock market positions." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Toolbars))

## Controversies

- "On July 31, 2009, The Laptop Company, Inc. stated in a press release that it would challenge Bing's trademark application, alleging that Bing may cause confusion in the marketplace as Bing and their product BongoBing both do online product search." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Legal_challenges))
- "Software company TeraByte Unlimited, which has a product called BootIt Next Generation (abbreviated to BING), also contended the trademark application on similar grounds, as did a Missouri-based design company called Bing!" ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Legal_challenges))
- "Microsoft contended that claims challenging its trademark were without merit because these companies filed for U.S. federal trademark applications only after Microsoft filed for the Bing trademark in March 2009." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Legal_challenges))
- "Microsoft has been criticized for censoring Bing search results to queries made in simplified Chinese characters which are used in mainland China." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Censorship_in_China))
- "This is done to comply with the censorship requirements of the government in China." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Censorship_in_China))
- "Microsoft has not indicated a willingness to stop censoring search results in simplified Chinese characters in the wake of Google's decision to do so." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bing#Censorship_in_China))
