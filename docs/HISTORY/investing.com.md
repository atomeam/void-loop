## Key milestones

- 2007: "The company was founded in 2007 by Israeli entrepreneur Dror Efrat, Russian entrepreneur Lonny Szneiberg and two other co-founders, initially under the name Forexpros." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2008: "During 2008–2009, more editions were added, and the platform expanded its offering from Forex data to encompass other financial instruments." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2008: "The company grew rapidly; Globes later attributed part of its growth to the 2008 financial crisis and heightened market volatility." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2012: "As of Q2 2012 Forexpros had 2.7 million unique visitors and 35 million pageviews per month, and a headcount of 70." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2012: "The company purchased its current domain, Investing.com, for $2.45 million in December 2012 in one of the most expensive domain-name sales at the time." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2013: "Investing.com's Android app was launched in September 2013, while an iOS version of the app was launched a year later." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2017: "Investing.com launched a new version of its cryptocurrency app in 2017." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2018: "In April 2018, Mickey Winitsky replaced Itay Gissin as CEO." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2018: "Winitsky announced an expansion strategy for 2018–19." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2018: "In July 2018, Investing.com signed a memorandum of understanding with TokenPost, a cryptocurrency news website in South Korea." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2012: "In late 2012, ForexPros was rebranded Investing.com." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- 2021: "In 2021, the company was acquired by Hong Kong–based Joffre Capital for $500 million." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
- "AllRates, launched a year earlier, was merged into Investing Money." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investing.com#History))
