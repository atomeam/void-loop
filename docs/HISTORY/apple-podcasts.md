## Key milestones

- 2005: "Apple was an early promoter of podcasts (the term is a portmanteau of Apple's iPod music player and "broadcast"), and added playback functionality to iTunes 4.9 released in June 2005 and built a directory of shows in its iTunes Music Store, starting with 3,000 entries." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Podcasts#History))
- 2020: "In April 2020, Apple Podcasts surpassed one million shows." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Podcasts#History))
- 2021: "In June 2021, Apple launched the option for podcast creators to implement paid subscriptions through podcast channels." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Podcasts#History))
