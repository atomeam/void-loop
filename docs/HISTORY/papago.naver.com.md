## Notable features

- "Papago ended its trial phase and officially launched on July 19, 2017, with translation options." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naver_Papago#Features))
- "It was only available as a smartphone app but it has since launched its own website and has expanded to other languages." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naver_Papago#Features))
- "From November 2019, Papago was changed to be available without Internet service." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naver_Papago#Features))
- "1:1 Conversation Mode: An interactive translation, translated through speech recognition." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naver_Papago#Main_functions))
- "Image Translation: The portion of a photo in a gallery or the characters in a newly photographed picture is specified and translated into text." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naver_Papago#Main_functions))
