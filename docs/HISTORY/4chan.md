Launched by Christopher "moot" Poole in October 2003, the site hosts boards dedicated to a wide variety of topics, including video games, television, literature, cooking, weapons, music, history, technology, anime, physical fitness, politics, and sports, among others. ## Key milestones

- 2008: "In 2008, 4chan was one of the Internet's busiest imageboards, according to the Los Angeles Times." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2008: "The 4chan servers were moved from Texas to California in August 2008, which upgraded the maximum bandwidth throughput of 4chan from 100 Mbit/s to 1 Gbit/s." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2009: "In January 2009, Poole signed a new deal with an advertising company; in February 2009, he was $20,000 in debt, and the site continued to lose money." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2010: "For instance, on December 28, 2010, 4chan and other websites went down due to such an attack, following which Poole said on his blog, "We now join the ranks of MasterCard, Visa, PayPal, et al. - an exclusive club!"" ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2011: "In a 2011 interview on Nico Nico Douga, Poole explained that there are approximately 20 volunteer moderators active on 4chan." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2022: "As of August 2022, the site's homepage lists 75 imageboards and one Flash animation board." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2022: "4chan's Alexa rank was 853 in March 2022, though it has been as high as 56." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
- 2024: "Gianluca Stringhini, an associate professor at Boston University College of Engineering, said in August 2024, "The only moderation on the platform appears to be for clearly illegal content, such as child pornography." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4chan#Background))
