## Key milestones

- 2001: "In 2001, Yahoo Finance launched a subscription service providing access to real-time stock quotes; quotes on the free service were provided on a 20-minute delay." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2005: "The site stopped using ticker information from Reuters in favor of direct feeds from major U.S. exchanges in 2005." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2012: "In 2012, Yahoo Finance began a content-sharing partnership with CNBC, following a similar partnership with ABC News the previous year." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2016: "In February 2016, Comscore reported that Yahoo Finance had 78 million monthly active users." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2017: "Yahoo Finance began providing live video content in 2017 and added more video programming in 2018." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2021: "Apollo Global Management acquired Yahoo in 2021, stating an aim to grow the Yahoo Finance business." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
- 2023: "In August 2023, Yahoo acquired CommonStock, a San Francisco-based social platform for retail investors to share insights based on their linked brokerage accounts." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Finance#History))
