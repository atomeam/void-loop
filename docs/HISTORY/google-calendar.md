## Notable features

- "Google Calendar allows users to create and edit events." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Features))
- "Events have a set start time and stop time, with an option for an "All-day event"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Features))
- "Users can enable a "Recurring" functionality with optional parameters for frequency." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Features))
- "Google Calendar entered a limited beta release on April 13, 2006, and exited the beta stage in July 2009." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Platforms))
- "Initially only available on the web and on the Android operating system, an iOS app was released on March 10, 2015." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Platforms))

## Controversies

- "In a March 2015 review of the Android app, Sarah Mitroff of CNET wrote that the new Material Design-inspired app was "cleaner", with "fewer distractions and colorful graphics that add a lot of personality to an otherwise dull app"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Reception))
- "She praised the Smart Suggestions feature for making new event creation easy, adding that "Even after just a few letters, the app will suggest the most relevant appointments." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Reception))
- "This is a great feature for creating events that occur over and over, like a haircut or doctor's appointment, because the app remembers the phrases you use"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Reception))
- "She complimented the integration between Google Calendar and Gmail, writing that "All of the relevant details are there, including confirmation numbers, links to the source email, even gate assignments for flights", and called it "one of my favorite parts of the app", but also noted that the feature could be turned off." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Calendar#Reception))
