## Key milestones

- 2008: "The company was founded by David Feller and Vadim Geshel in early 2008." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))
- 2013: "In March 2013, Yummly opened access to its application programming interface to other companies as a paid service." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))
- 2014: "In 2014, Yummly had 15 million active users in the US and launched international websites in the UK, Germany and The Netherlands." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))
- 2024: "On 3rd April 2024, website TheSpoon reported that the entire Yummly team had been made redundant." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))
- 2024: "The app and website were taken offline in December 2024, with the latter pointing to KitchenAid's US website." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2017: "In May 2017, the company was acquired by appliance maker Whirlpool Corporation and allowed to operate as a subsidiary, keeping its current head office." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#History))

## Controversies

- "In December 2014, Yummly was named by Apple as one of the "Best of 2014" apps in the App Store." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#Reception))
- "In March 2022, PCMag gave Yummly a rating of 3.0 out of 5, praising the app for its "useful recipe-collecting and grocery-shopping tools, as well as cool video lessons," but noting that the app had "numerous issues, including the inability to edit recipes, the absence of a digital pantry, and poor grocery data consolidation," comparing it negatively with rival meal-planning app Paprika." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yummly#Reception))
