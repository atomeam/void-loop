## Notable features

- "Bauer writes about recipes for popular dishes such as meatloaf and apple pie, as well as how-to articles on subjects such as how to cook the perfect boiled egg or how to best caramelize an onion." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Content))
- "The blog often features guest bloggers." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Content))
- "Each recipe is attached with a photo of the final product, along with several photos that portray each major step in the recipe." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Content))

## Controversies

- "By January 2007, Simply Recipes was receiving over one million visits per month and had over 500 recipes." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Reception))
- "The Well Fed Network, a compilation of food blogs, awarded Simply Recipes the award for best blog overall of 2006." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Reception))
- "In 2010, Forbes magazine listed Bauer among "Eight Of The Very Best Food Bloggers." Bauer's blog has been described as taking a "relaxed approach" to food." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Reception))
- "Maggie Wrobel of The Globe and Mail appreciated the website's method of organizing recipes in categories, making it easier to find recipes that she was interested in." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_Recipes#Reception))
