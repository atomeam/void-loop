## Key milestones

- 2011: "iCloud was announced on May 31, 2011, in a press release." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2016: "In 2016, Apple replaced Azure with Google Cloud Platform." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2019: "In June 2019, iCloud was introduced to Windows 10 via the Microsoft Store." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2021: "In 2021, The Information reported that Apple was storing 8 million TB of data on Google's cloud, and was on track to spend $300 million that year." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2021: "In June 2021, Apple introduced iCloud+, which added Private Relay, Hide My Email and Custom Email Domain to paid users of the services, as well as an unlimited storage limit for video from cameras added through HomeKit Secure Video." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2022: "In March 2022, Apple settled a class-action lawsuit alleging that it had misled users by storing data on non-Apple servers." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2025: "In February 2025, iCloud+ introduced a new app called Apple Invites." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))

## Ownership changes, pivots and shutdowns

- 2011: "On June 6, 2011, during the WWDC 2011 keynote, Steve Jobs announced that iCloud would replace MobileMe, which had been widely seen as a "failure", a fact which Steve Jobs acknowledged during the announcement. iCloud was released on October 12, 2011, and MobileMe was discontinued on June 30, 2012." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2016: "On January 28, 2016, ad-free iTunes Radio was discontinued and is therefore no longer part of iTunes Match." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#iTunes_Match))
- 2019: "It was discontinued on July 1, 2019. iCloud had 20 million users within a week after launch." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#History))
- 2023: "In May 2023, Apple announced the discontinuation of Photo Stream, with uploads turned off on June 26, and the service turned off on July 26." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Photo_Stream))

## Notable features

- "iCloud is a free service, and comes with 5 GB of cloud storage." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Features))
- "Users can subscribe to iCloud+ for additional storage up to 12 TB (or 14 TB for Apple One Premier plan users who receive an additional 2 TB of storage as part of the plan)." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Features))
- "Some of iCloud's features are accessible not only through apps built into iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, tvOS and macOS but also on iCloud.com." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Features))
- "In September 2026, Apple began bundling the Apple TV streaming service, Apple Arcade, and a limited version of Apple Music with all paid iCloud+ subscriptions in more than 100 countries, primarily smaller territories or those deemed to have greater price sensitivity such as India." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Bundled_services_in_select_countries))
- "The Apple One bundle will also be wound down in all applicable countries where it was previously available." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Bundled_services_in_select_countries))

## Controversies

- "In 2013, as part of the Snowden revelations, The Washington Post and The Guardian reported on leaked NSA documents which showed that iCloud was part of the NSA's PRISM surveillance program, along with other cloud services." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Security_and_privacy))
- "According to the documents, the NSA could access emails, chats, photos and videos, and stored files." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Security_and_privacy))
- "The documents specifically stated that the data was collected through "equipment installed at company-controlled locations"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Security_and_privacy))
- "The Washington Post further stated that Apple, like the other companies, was aware of the program and was a willing participant." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICloud#Security_and_privacy))
