## Key milestones

- 2011: "Any.do originally launched on Android in November 2011 and within 30 days, reached over 500,000 downloads and 40 million users by 2022." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Any.do#History))
- 2016: "In 2016 Any.do added a built-in calendar, a freemium subscription model Any.do Premium and AI Assistant software that offers to complete some tasks on demand." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Any.do#History))
- 2023: "In 2023, Any.do launched its team collaboration tier for teams named Any.do Workspace." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Any.do#History))
- 2024: "In 2024, Any.do launched its offering for families tier named Any.do for Families." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Any.do#History))
