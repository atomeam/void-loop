## Key milestones

- 2008: "Simplenote was originally developed by Simperium in 2008." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplenote#History))
- 2009: "Simplenote Premium was introduced on November 23, 2009." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplenote#History))
- 2013: "Automattic acquired Simperium and Simplenote on January 24, 2013." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplenote#History))
- 2016: "In May 2016, an official client for Linux for Simplenote was released." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplenote#History))
- 2016: "Automattic open sourced code for its Android, Electron, iOS, and macOS Simplenote apps in August 2016, making all its client apps open source." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplenote#History))
