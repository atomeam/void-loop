## Notable features

- "Inbox by Gmail scanned the user's incoming Gmail messages for information." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Features))
- "It gathered email messages related to the same overall topic into an organized bundle, with a title describing the bundle's content." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Features))
- "For example, flight tickets, car rentals, and hotel reservations were grouped under "Travel", giving the user an easier overview of emails." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Features))
- "Inbox by Gmail was announced on a limited invitation-only basis on October 22, 2014, available on the web, and through the Android and iOS mobile operating systems." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Platforms))
- "It was officially released to the public on May 28, 2015." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Platforms))

## Controversies

- "David Pierce of The Verge praised the service, writing that it was "minimalist and lovely, full of layers and easy to navigate." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Reception))
- "It's remarkably fast and smooth on all platforms, and far better on iOS than the Gmail app"." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Reception))
- "However, he criticized the app's low density of information, with only a few emails visible on the screen at a time, making it "a bit of a challenge" for users who need to go through "hundreds of emails" every day." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Reception))
- "Although positive that "Inbox feels a lot like the future of email", Pierce wrote that there was "plenty of algorithm tweaking and design condensing to do", with particular attention needed on a "compact view" for denser view of information on the screen." ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbox_by_Gmail#Reception))
