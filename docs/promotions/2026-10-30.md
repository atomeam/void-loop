# Skill promotions, 2026-10-30

Verse asked for the list of its checked and tested skills that can be promoted to proven. This file is that list, with the decision and the evidence for each skill. It uses the standard in [2026-10-29.md](2026-10-29.md): a skill is proven when `tools/families.mjs` rates it proven from evidence only. No skill was moved by hand.

## What changed since 2026-10-29

The 2026-10-29 record held most skills for "routing gaps". That count came from a benchmark probe that waited 4 s for each answer while the machine was under load. A probe with a 15 s wait (`VOID_BENCH_ASK_WAIT_MS=15000 node tools/bench.mjs --probe`) showed that most of those asks do reach the skill. Every ask added to `tools/bench.json` below passed that probe on this branch: 29 of 38 candidate asks passed, and 4 more probes for night-sky and magic8 all missed.

## Result

17 skills were below proven on main (ef1f379c7). 11 now meet the standard. 6 do not, and they stay where the evidence puts them. All skills: 371 proven, 5 checked, 1 tested (was 360, 15, 2).
