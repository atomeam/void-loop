# Skill promotions, 2026-10-29

Adam asked to promote the checked and tested skills to proven, starting with the Words and the world family. The owner review set the rule: promote only the skills that meet the proven standard, and record the evidence, the date and the decision for each one. This file is that record.

## The standard

A skill is proven when tools/families.mjs rates it proven from evidence only:

1. A reproducible test that passes: the skill's own unit test (`tools/units.test.mjs` and the per-skill test files), which checks its examples, refuses its near misses and checks exact results.
2. Regression coverage: at least 3 real asks in `tools/bench.json` that route to the skill and pass. A browser probe (`node tools/bench.mjs --probe`) proved each new ask before it was added.
3. Or a third kind of evidence (the browser suite or a live finding) beside the first two.

No skill was moved by hand. The ratings below are what `node tools/families.mjs` computes from the evidence on this branch.

## Result

66 skills were below proven on main (2942566d2 and later). 44 now meet the standard. 22 do not, and they stay where the evidence puts them.
