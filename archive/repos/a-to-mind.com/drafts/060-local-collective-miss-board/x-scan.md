What changed in the window

No new lab release in the sampled posts. The conversation is spelling, package size, a video cast, and a double claim. The joke out-engaged the proof posts. A 2026-07-06 PLOS Biology paper on shared false memories (Shao, Chen, Loftus, Zhu) is context from outside the X sample, not a post in this window.

Who is saying it

Personal accounts. No memory-lab account in the sample. @Sanatan_Prabhat used "collective memory" for a historical-remembrance appeal; that is a different use of the phrase and is not a miss claim.

Disconfirming evidence

- Penney: a 1958 primary that spells the store Penny's as the legal name, or a filing that shows a rename. A diary spelling does not settle it. This page does not fetch the filing.
- Can size: a sealed label archive. A shelf today is not a past.
- Video cast: the original upload hash. A memory of a blonde boy is not a retcon seal.
- Double claim: a primary death record plus a chain of custody for identity. Repetition of the claim is not that chain.
- Shared-miss definition: a pre-registered count that survives after people are shown the fixture first. Agreement after social exposure does not confirm an external editor.

Hypothesis (design material)

A recalled public wording can sit beside a local fixture as a hashed miss. Disagreement is data. It is not a timeline proof, a double, or a collective seal.

Confidence: 0.22. Unattested default.

Observation / claim / inference

Observation: several posts offer a personal recall as proof or as a retcon, and one defines the effect as a shared false memory. Claim (inside the posts): the public record changed, or a person was replaced. Inference for Void: store the recall, the fixture key, and a disconfirm line; never promote a local count to attested.
