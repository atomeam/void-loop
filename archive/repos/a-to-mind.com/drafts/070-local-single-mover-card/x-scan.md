## Design inference (not a finding)

The UAP posts and the co-agency posts both collapse a narrator's attribution into an event: a clip becomes a craft, a chat turn becomes a shared decision. The interface draft refuses that collapse. A step can be hashed only after exactly one mover is sealed. "Both" stays denied. Machine text is paste, not an instruction. Nothing is sent.

One-line hypothesis for the draft: a single sealed mover is a hashed hypothesis of attribution, not a handoff and not a shared mind. Confidence as design-material weight: 0.30. Default status: unattested.
