## What existing implementations do

Public co-agency surfaces treat a shared step as permission to act.

- Horvitz's mixed-initiative framing, restated by Holter, Moruzzi, and El-Assady in "Towards agency in human-AI collaboration" (IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications, 2026), asks how agency is shared and dynamically allocated. The paper reflects on visual mixed-initiative systems, including speculative preview. Allocation is a design choice, not a measurement that a centaur formed.
- Ouilhet Olmos, "The Interface Is Downstream" (arXiv:2609.28801, submitted 2026-09-23), argues that permissions, evidence rules, and retrieval already shape an agent before it replies, and that a retrieved synthesis can be repeated without its chain. That is a provenance caution, not a seal that consent traveled upstream.
- Contact-center orchestration (Twilio's 2026-09-25 agent-orchestration note; NHI Mgmt Group's 2026-09-30 handoff FAQ) separates routing from approval, then still ships a handoff tool the model can call. The FAQ says review placed after the consequential decision is ceremonial. The tool call is the thing Void does not ship.
- Insurance-agent tutorials switch a session to manual mode after the model calls `initiate_human_handoff`. The model still chooses the moment.
- Posts in this window sell the centaur ("man and machine greater than either alone"), frictionless agent-to-agent steps with no person on each step, and a video Turing-test blur. The outperform claim and the blur sit inside the claim.
