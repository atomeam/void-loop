## Hypothesis, not a finding

A shared wrong recall is a hypothesis about memory, not a record and not a chorus. Laboratory work already treats a lure word that was never on a list (the DRM paradigm) and a popular image that many people misdescribe (Prasad and Bainbridge, 2022) as schema completion, not as a catalog edit. A July 2026 PLOS Biology paper reports that misinformation can raise the chance of shared false memories of event details, with similar dorsomedial prefrontal patterns while people read the misinformation. An ICLR 2026 slide deck reports that multi-agent LLM groups can also lock a false detail, and that larger role-based groups sometimes become suspicious of perfect agreement. None of that is a sealed record, and none of it is a chorus this page may store.

Public posts in this window treat a shared miss as gaslight-denial, as a licence metaphor, as a possible TV implant, or as an AI edit called a "real time Mandela effect." Void does not count those posts, does not keep the recalled sentence, and does not attest a timeline change.

## What existing sites do

Mandela-effect boards collect a recalled spelling or logo, then show how many people share it. Quiz pages score the miss. Multi-agent demos report a reality-shift rate. All three collapse an echo into a record or a chorus.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. No echo class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`.
- Catalog only: `withheld`, `residue-label`, `detail-lure`, `source-slip`, `abstain`, `local-only`. Free text is not a class.
- The radio is the opt-in. It is not a poll and not a memory grant.
- Seal hashes `echo-not-record|v1|<class>|record-refusal|status=unattested` with SHA-256. The full digest is the record-refusal id.
- The record sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. A disconfirm line is kept off the hash.
- The record-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.
- No poll, no vote store, no percentage, no chorus badge, no canon edit, no shared-memory write, no image of a logo.
- Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them.
- Static-friendly. Optional Worker refuses `/record`, `/chorus`, `/canon`, and `/poll` with 403 and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`.
