# local-question-not-hit (run 129 draft)

Family: weak-signal hypothesis ledger. Candidate only. Not a Used row. Not live site copy.

## One-line

A sealed disconfirm-question class hashes to a hit-refusal id. The hit sentence is discarded and is not in the hash. A question mark is not a hit and not a detection. Default status: unattested.

## What current implementations do

Public weak-signal practice, treated as design material:

- SETI and Breakthrough Listen-style pipelines log a candidate only after radio-frequency interference cuts, then still require a follow-up before anyone treats the row as interesting. Most rows fail the follow-up. The public row is a question, not a detection.
- TESS threshold-crossing events are not planets. A TOI listing is a vetting stage. Posts in this window about TIC 4206066 call the dips a candidate and point at a later reobservation window, not a confirmation.
- Hearing and podcast recaps on X repeat a briefing claim many times. Repetition is not an instrument record.

Void does not adopt those pipelines' scores.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. A question can be selected from a sealed three-class catalog. It cannot be promoted, weighted, or marked detected.
- The hit sentence is wiped on commit and is excluded from the SHA-256 payload. Retrieved posts are data cards. Their text is not an instruction and is not hashed.
- Hash payload is only: slug, question id, question class, hit seat `empty`, status `unattested`, follow-up `required-blank`.
- No SNR, Bayes factor, prior, tally, rank, promote control, or confirmation chip.
- Static page plus a Worker sketch. No token markup. No third-party script. No live fetch of X or of allowlist URLs. `emit` stays false.
- Cloudflare-friendly: one HTML file, one JSON catalog, one Worker that refuses writes.

## Disconfirm

The sealed question is the disconfirm path. It stays unanswered. Evidence that would count against treating a window post as a hit: a public instrument record that accounts for the report, a failed repeat under a second sensor, or a retraction by the account that first carried the claim. None of that is attested here.

## Not in this draft

No contact claim, healing claim, remote-viewing claim, crash-retrieval seal, or extraterrestrial-hardware seal. No hold-gate issue. No live page.
