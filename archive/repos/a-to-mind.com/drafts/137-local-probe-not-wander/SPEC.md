# local-probe-not-wander — run 137 draft

## One-line

Local probe-not-wander; a sealed probe class hashes to a wander-refusal id; the wander sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a probe mark is not a wander score and not a mind-wandering diagnosis; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Family: attention/interoception. Design material only. A public post or a lab caption can treat a thought probe, a body-awareness line, or a percent of waking hours as a wander score already read off attention. This draft treats that promotion as a hypothesis. It does not write "we now know." Status defaults to unattested. Confidence in `x-scan.md` is a design-material weight, not a measurement.

Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a seat and they do not enter the hash.

## What existing sites do

Experience-sampling probes ask a person to class a moment as on-task, mind-wandering, or mind-blanking, then pair the class with physiology. A 2025 Communications Biology report of a sustained-attention-to-response task in 65 adults (EEG, ECG, pupil) describes probe rates on the order of 49% on-task, 39% mind-wandering, and 13% mind-blanking, and links wandering to heart-rate variability and a late heartbeat-evoked potential, with blanking tied to lower heart rate and pupil size. Those are lab claims about cooperative sessions. They are not a seal that a probe mark on this page is a wander score. This draft does not record ECG, EEG, or pupil size.

A 2025 Brain and Behavior systematic review of mind-wandering methods says questionnaires and experience sampling are common and biased, that imaging is costly and low in ecological validity, and that no single method captures the subtypes. A separate trait study reports that negative automatic thoughts predict interoceptive attention until trait anxiety is in the model, and that a state probe of thoughts versus body versus task can show competition in one moment. A 2026 Psychophysiology paper argues that a two-alternative heartbeat-tone task is a poor test of cardiac interoceptive accuracy. Those limits are design material. This page does not score accuracy.

Public posts in this window do four versions. An assistant reply cites a 35–47% wandering band and turns it into hours lost. Another assistant reply says deep meditation heightens interoception until internal sounds feel like a cosmic rhythm. A clinic caption lists body awareness next to mobility. A note says anxious attention can make an ordinary heartbeat seem alarming, and a separate note says body sensations labeled ascension overlap ordinary stress and are not diagnostic. An idiom, a fiction stage direction, and an oddball-blip essay sit beside those claims and are not results. This draft does not score them.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No probe class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. There is no control that flips status to attested.

Catalog only: `withheld`, `unprobed`, `mark-only`, `off-task-blank`, `no-score`, `abstain`. Free text is not a class. One probe seat. The wander seat is locked empty. Nothing inserts a percent, a BPM, an HRV number, or an arousal gauge.

Hash preimage, only: `probe-not-wander|v1|<class>|wander-refusal|status=unattested`. SHA-256. The full digest is the wander-refusal id. The wander sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, percents, BPM, HRV, and pupil sizes are kept off the hash. The wander-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.

No camera, no microphone, no PPG, no ECG, no pupil tracker, no EEG, no experience-sampling percent, no BPM field, no HRV score, no arousal gauge, no body-map diagnosis, no wander badge, no promote control. The radio is the opt-in. It is not a sampling grant.

Static-friendly. Optional Worker refuses `/wander`, `/score`, `/percent`, `/arousal`, `/heartbeat`, and `/diagnosis` with 403 and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`. No network. No clipboard. No storage. No live page write. Company context stays outside this draft: Void Monthly $49 and the glass-box ledger are not a probe result.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The probe would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent experience-sampling record into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page writing a wander score. A pre-registered empty wander seat that stays empty when no second sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. A percent that names a mind-wandering diagnosis is the behavior it refuses. A heartbeat task that fails as an accuracy test is a lab limit this draft does not copy and does not need.
