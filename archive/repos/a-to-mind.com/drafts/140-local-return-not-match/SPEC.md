## One-line

Local return-not-match; a sealed return class hashes to a match-refusal id; the match sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a return mark is not a match and not a solved incubation; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

A set-aside can be left, and a later note can be labeled with a closed class. That label is not evidence the later note solves the set-aside. Sleep-onset work (Lacaux et al., Science Advances 2021) reported more hidden-rule discoveries after at least 15 seconds of N1 than after wake, on one number-reduction task, and reported the gain absent in N2. Sio and Ormerod (2009) treated incubation as a moderated break effect, with forgetting fixation as a leading account. Sanders, Beeman, and Paller (2021) reported an interaction between overnight cues and fixating information and inferred processing beyond simple forgetting. Those are hypotheses for the card. They are not a match score and not a morning yield.

## What existing sites do

Incubation pages and device notes often turn a break into a solution: a hypnagogic window becomes a scene to replay, a wearable cue becomes dream content, a morning fragment becomes the answer that was incubating. Targeted-dream writeups pair a waking cue with a sleep-onset prompt and treat the later report as incorporation. Creativity posts treat an unconscious break as insight. None of that is sealed here.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No return class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. Catalog is only `withheld`, `blank`, `fragment`, `unrelated`, `same-words`, `abstain`. `same-words` is a closed label. It does not store the words and does not compute overlap.

One return seat. The match seat stays locked empty. Nothing on the page inserts a match score, insight badge, dream scene, sleep stage, cue, or yield. The hash preimage is `return-not-match|v1|<class>|match-refusal|status=unattested`, SHA-256. The match sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, paper names, and cue names stay off the hash. The match-refusal id changes only if the sealed class changes.

No audio cue, sleep tracker, dream-report recorder, Dormio link, countdown, bottle-drop timer, lucidity score, image field, insight badge, match score, scene replay, or morning-yield badge. The radio is an opt-in class, not a sleep grant and not a solution grant.

The paste well accepts retrieved text as data. Lines that look like instructions are flagged, not executed, and not hashed. Empty allowlist. No fetch.

Static-friendly. Optional Worker sketch refuses `/match`, `/dream`, `/insight`, `/scene`, `/yield`, `/cue`, `/sleep`, `/audio`, and `/score` with 403 and stores nothing. Not deployed.

No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No network. No clipboard. No storage. No live-page write.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

A pre-registered blind comparison of a sealed set-aside with a later note, against a no-break control, that a human seals on another ledger. A replication in which the N1 association on the number-reduction task does not appear. A morning sentence a reader calls related is not that record. If a lab result and a sealed return class carried the same information as a match judgment, the design weight of this card would drop. It would still not attest a solved incubation.
