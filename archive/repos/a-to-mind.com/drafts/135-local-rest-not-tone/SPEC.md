# local-rest-not-tone — run 135 draft

## One-line

Local rest-not-tone; a sealed rest class hashes to a tone-refusal id; the tone sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a rest mark is not a tone and not a carrier lock; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Family: quiet-signal filter. Design material only. A public post can treat a quiet interval, a name, or a filter hit as a tone (carrier, beacon, Wow, or confirmed signal). This draft treats that promotion as a hypothesis. It does not write "we now know." Status defaults to unattested. Confidence in `x-scan.md` is a design-material weight, not a measurement.

Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a seat and they do not enter the hash.

## What existing sites do

Amateur squelch and CTCSS gates stay closed until a carrier or a code clears a floor, then open as if someone is there. Breakthrough Listen narrowband pipelines (turboSETI-style drift search, SNR threshold on the order of 10, ON–OFF cadence) and kurtosis block cuts (ON median kurtosis at least 0.5, OFF at most 0.25 in the 2025 Green Bank archive search) still hand a shortlist to a human. A 2025 anomaly-ranking pass on Parkes and Green Bank spectrograms inspected on the order of 20,000 candidates and reported that none survived basic scrutiny. Those filters improve a queue. They do not make a rest into a tone. The 1977 Wow intensity excursion remains a named non-repeat, not a sealed detection.

Public posts in this window do three versions. A detector note says a frozen unit on a constructed null still marked 992 of 1,024 pairs as strong witnesses. A labeling note separates confirmed, suspected, and unknown, and refuses to guess a cause from missing data. A video caption uses the Wow name for views and likes. Those interfaces can still be read as a lift. This draft does not lift.

Earlier Void drafts in this family already refused other moves: `local-quiet-signal-filter` (residue against a floor), `local-underfloor-stub` (typed amplitude under a floor), `local-closed-gate` (closed gate), `local-hush-not-speech` (hush is not speech), `local-squelch-not-lift` (squelch is not a lift), `local-bin-not-spike` (bin is not a spike). This draft does not reuse those slugs and does not draw their spike badge, SNR number, FFT, microphone, or promote control.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No rest class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. There is no control that flips status to attested.

Catalog only: `withheld`, `below-floor`, `gate-closed`, `no-repeat`, `rest-empty`, `abstain`. Free text is not a class. One rest seat. The tone seat is locked empty. Nothing inserts a frequency, a level, or an open gate.

Hash preimage, only: `rest-not-tone|v1|<class>|tone-refusal|status=unattested`. SHA-256. The full digest is the tone-refusal id. The tone sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, frequencies, SNR figures, and any typed carrier name are kept off the hash. The tone-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.

No microphone, no AudioContext, no FFT, no frequency field, no SNR number, no squelch-open control, no carrier-lock badge, no tone badge, no promote control. The radio is the opt-in. It is not a lock grant.

Static-friendly. Optional Worker refuses `/tone`, `/carrier`, `/lock`, `/squelch-open`, and `/detect` with 403 and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`. No network. No clipboard. No storage. No live page write.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The rest would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent repeat into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page opening a gate. An ON–OFF file that fails a pre-registered floor would be evidence about a scan claim, not a reason for this page to draw a tone. A pre-registered empty tone seat that stays empty when no second sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. A caption that names a carrier is the behavior it refuses.
