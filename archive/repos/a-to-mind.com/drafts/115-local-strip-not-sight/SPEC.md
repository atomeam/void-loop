# local-strip-not-sight — run 115 draft

Family: sensory substitution. Candidate only. Not a Used row. Not a seal.

## One-line

Local strip-not-sight; a sealed strip class hashes to a sight-refusal id; the sight sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a strip mark is not sight and not a recovered scene; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Visual-to-auditory substitution devices scan an image as a strip or column and map that strip onto sound. The vOICe (Meijer 1992) sweeps left to right, mapping height to pitch and brightness to loudness. EyeMusic (Abboud et al. 2014) uses instrument timbre as a color stand-in. A 2025 review of sonification methods (EPJ Special Topics) sorts these into column sweeps, whole-image complexes, and depth sonification, and notes sensory-cognitive overload as an open cost. Public posts in this window still slide from a mapping into "a new way to see," including when a device account quotes a goal that is not restored normal sight.

Void does not pick a device, does not play a soundscape, and does not attest functional vision. It seals the strip class and refuses the sight sentence.

Sources are design material, not attested mechanism:

- Meijer, P. B. L. (1992). An experimental system for auditory image representations. IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering.
- Abboud, S., Hanassy, S., Levy-Tzedek, S., Maidenbaum, S., and Amedi, A. (2014). EyeMusic: Introducing a "visual" colorful experience for the blind using auditory sensory substitution. Restorative Neurology and Neuroscience.
- Ward, J., and Meijer, P. (2010). Visual experiences in the blind induced by an auditory sensory substitution device. Consciousness and Cognition. Phenomenology reports are data, not a seal of restored sight.
- Review hypothesis only: image-sonification methods, traditional and AI-assisted, EPJ Special Topics (2025), 234:3605–3627.

Do not write that a strip is sight, that a soundscape recovers a scene, or that a mapping equals an implant.

## What existing sites do

The vOICe web app and EyeMusic take a camera frame, sonify a sweep, and present the result as image-like information, sometimes next to implant comparisons. Training sites score object recognition. Fringe posts collapse the sweep into "seeing." Both treat the output as the scene.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. No strip class is selected. Status stays `unattested`.
- Catalog only: `withheld`, `blank`, `marked`. Free text is not a class.
- Seal hashes `void-strip-not-sight|v1|class=<id>|status=unattested` with SHA-256. The first 16 hex chars are the sight-refusal id.
- The sight sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage.
- No camera, no `getUserMedia`, no microphone, no Web Audio oscillator, no sonification playback, no image-to-sound map, no tongue array, no implant-equivalence score, no functional-vision badge.
- The strip is eight unlabeled cells. A mark is a filled cell, not an object name.
- Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them.
- Static-friendly. Optional Worker echoes a refusal and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`.
