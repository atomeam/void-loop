## What current implementations do

Speech brain-computer interfaces treat neural activity during attempted or imagined speech as a word stream. A 2025 Stanford / BrainGate report, covered by the New York Times on 2025-08-14, decoded cued imagined sentences from motor cortex in four clinical-trial participants and reported about 74 percent accuracy on a 125,000-word vocabulary. The same group trained a password ("chittychittybangbang") so the decoder would not treat private inner speech as output. That is a lexical decode with a wake gate. It is not a mark, and the password does not prove the person meant the sentence.

A 2025 phenomenology paper (Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences, 2025-01-20) argues that communication BCIs do not read a hidden Cartesian mind. They sit on part of an embodied speaking process. Rathkopf-style "mind reading" — predicting content from neural data that is not a conventional symbol — is a different claim from predicting from words or body language. The paper does not attest either.

Non-speaking communication claims in public culture often collapse a mark into a received sentence. The Telepathy Tapes podcast says nonspeaking people communicate by spelling and, in the title claim, telepathically. Critics in Skeptical Inquirer (2025-05-29 and 2026-08) and Science-Based Medicine (2025-02-05) say the tests did not separate facilitator cueing from authorship, and that facilitated communication failed controlled authorship tests in the 1990s. ASHA has position statements against facilitated communication and letter-board variants. A helper's letter is not the other person's mark.

In the 2026-10-03/04 window, @directRmarketer said the point was expressions, not words. @nameshapes said communication via telepathy and intent is more efficient. @amaralexsandra said it felt like people were speaking in their head. @TimesUpThanks described an unspoken observation echoed by someone else. @nrol_ling said a word does not need a meaning for a judgment about the speaker. @algxtradingx said a spontaneously heard voice should not be classed as divine communication, and that a speech generator has not shown that it hears. @EPallazo69140 claimed abductees felt mind-controlling telepathy. Those are posts, not traces.

Void does not ship the decoder, the letter board, the facilitator, or the received-mind badge.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. The page is one static file. It does not fetch posts, does not read the clipboard, and does not open a microphone, camera, implant, or sensor. No `getUserMedia`. No WebSocket. Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The X scan in this folder is not loaded by the page.

A note is a hashed hypothesis:

- The mark alphabet is five sealed non-letters from `claims.json` embedded in the page: dot, bar, arc, gap, press. They are not a font, not letters, and not a navigation gesture. Run 059 already drafted gaze and chord input. This page does not look, does not dwell, and does not steer.
- A sequence needs at least two marks. One tap cannot hash. The sequence is the residue. It is not a sentence.
- The gloss is optional and labeled "later word, not the mark." Hashing does not promote the gloss into the mark. An empty gloss is allowed.
- Source is required and sealed: self-noted, overheard-claim, or machine-decode-claim. None of the three sets `received`. Received is locked to `not-claimed`. Authorship is locked to `not-verified`. Mind-read is locked to `denied`. Status defaults to `unattested`. The page cannot set `attested`.
- Gates, all required before a hash: two or more marks, a source, and a disconfirm sentence. A single pass cannot hash.
- Confidence is a design-material weight the person types, capped as a label, not a measurement.
- Opt-in `sessionStorage` only. Default is memory. Closing the tab drops the note.
- No token markup. No `{{ }}` slots. No third-party script. Cloudflare can serve the file as static. The worker sketch refuses every write.

This sits beside the glass-box ledger as a local hypothesis note. It is not a Void Monthly checkout, not a resumable run, and not live copy.

## What would count as disconfirming

A second pass whose mark sequence does not match the first. A gloss the person withdraws. A source correction from machine-decode-claim to self-noted. A primary in which the imagined-speech decoder fails the password gate, which would still not make this mark a received mind. A controlled authorship test that separates a helper from a nonspeaking person, which this page does not run. None of those promote the row to attested.
