# local-gap-not-bridge

Run 122 candidate. Family: anomalous-event timeline. Status: draft, not used, not sealed. Queue was empty. This file does not change live site copy.

## One-line

Local gap-not-bridge; two sealed class seats and a locked empty gap seat hash to a bridge-refusal id; the bridge sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a gap mark is not a bridge and not a missing event; unattested default.

## What current implementations do

Public chronology pages treat a dated index as a finding aid and then invite the reader to see clusters, repeated motifs, and cases connected by period. A research chronology can say dates may be exact, approximate, traditional, or publication-based, and still describe the timeline as useful for spotting connections. That second step is the bridge.

Diagnostic event logs do the other common thing: they keep timestamps and then infer the link between adjacent rows (screen, action, network, persistence). The inferred link is the product. A screenshot is called incomplete precisely because it lacks that bridge.

Public posts in the run 122 scan do a third version. A hearing note is placed on a calendar and read as the next step in a disclosure sequence. Year marks (2011, 2017, 2027) are read as a span with a meaning in the gap. A case write-up walks from lights, to a memo, to a later symbol claim, and the walk itself is the plot. A mosaic note warns against assigning every residual to one hand, which is the disconfirming shape this draft keeps: the empty seat stays empty.

Earlier Void drafts in this family already refused other moves: `local-anomaly-timeline` (dated note), `local-unfilled-span` (unfilled span), `local-sort-not-arc` (sort is not an arc), `local-cluster-not-wave` (cluster is not a wave), `local-stamp-not-plot` (stamp is not a plot or a chronology). This draft does not reuse those slugs and does not draw their date axis, connecting line, wave badge, flap score, era name, sequence id, or next/prev control.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. No emit, no seal, no network, no clipboard, no storage, no live page write.
- Three seats only. Left and right accept a sealed class token. The middle seat is locked to `gap-empty`. A filled-gap control is present and disabled. Nothing inserts an event into the gap.
- The bridge sentence is local scratch. It is cleared from the hash payload before digest. It is not a field in `claims.json`.
- Hash payload is the class tokens plus the unattested status and the bridge-refusal marker. SHA-256 via `crypto.subtle`. No token markup.
- Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a seat and they do not enter the hash.
- Cloudflare/static-friendly. One HTML file. Optional Worker sketch returns the sealed contract on GET and 403 on every write. It does not fetch posts.
- Unattested default. There is no control that flips status to attested.
- A gap mark is not a bridge and not a missing event. It is also not a chronology, a flap, a wave, a synchronicity, contact, healing, remote viewing, crash retrieval, or extraterrestrial hardware.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The gap seat would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent record into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page inferring the link. A published hearing notice with a witness list would be evidence about a calendar claim in the scan, not a reason for this page to draw a line. A pre-registered empty interval that stays empty when no sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. A narrative that names the missing middle is the behavior it refuses.
