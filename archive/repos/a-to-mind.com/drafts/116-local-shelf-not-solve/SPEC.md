## Hypothesis, not a finding

Incubation accounts set a problem aside and later treat a sleep-onset residue, a hypnagogic image, or a morning narrative as the solution. Wallas (1926) named incubation as a stage between preparation and illumination. A 2009 meta-analysis (Sio and Ormerod, Psychological Bulletin) treated an incubation interval as associated with later solution rates, with task type and interval length as moderators, not as a mechanism seal. Lacaux and colleagues (Science Advances, 2021) reported that at least 15 seconds in N1 during a rest tripled discovery of a hidden number-reduction rule in their sample (83% versus 30% who stayed awake), and that the association vanished in deeper sleep. A Science perspective dated 2026-10-07 restates that protocol and still frames an object-drop as a way to catch a creative spark. Public posts in this window slide from a hypnagogic description into a desired scene, a dimension claim, or a productivity sentence.

Void does not run a nap, does not store a dream report, and does not attest a solution. It seals the shelf class and refuses the solve sentence.

Sources are design material, not attested mechanism:

- Wallas, G. (1926). The Art of Thought. Incubation is a named stage, not a solved problem.
- Sio, U. N., and Ormerod, T. C. (2009). Does incubation enhance problem solving? A meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin, 135(1), 94–120. An interval association is not a cause seal.
- Lacaux, C., et al. (2021). Sleep onset is a creative sweet spot. Science Advances, 7(50), eabj5866. A reported N1 association in one protocol is not a shelf that solves.
- Horowitz, A. H., et al. targeted-dream-incubation notes (design material only): an audio cue at sleep onset plus a later report is still a report, not a verified solution.

Do not write that a shelf is a solution, that a hypnagogic image is a retrieved answer, or that a dream narrative is insight.

## What existing sites do

Dream journals store a free-text report and tag lucidity or meaning. Edison-style capture pages time an object drop or a countdown, then ask for the image as the idea. Manifestation copy tells the reader to replay a desired scene as analytical filters loosen. All three treat the return text as the solve.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. No shelf class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`.
- Catalog only: `withheld`, `empty`, `shelved`. Free text is not a class.
- Seal hashes `void-shelf-not-solve|v1|class=<id>|status=unattested` with SHA-256. The first 16 hex chars are the solve-refusal id.
- The solve sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage.
- No audio cue, no sleep tracker, no dream-report recorder, no bottle-drop timer, no countdown, no lucidity score, no image field, no insight badge.
- The shelf is three closed slots. A shelved mark fills one slot. The slot has no label and no scene.
- Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them.
- Static-friendly. Optional Worker echoes a refusal and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`.
