# local-grain-not-picture — run 139 draft

## One-line

Local grain-not-picture; a sealed grain class hashes to a picture-refusal id; the picture sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a grain mark is not a picture and not a recovered scene; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Family: sensory substitution. Design material only. A public post or a device caption can treat a substituted grain as already a picture: a soundscape becomes functional sight, a phosphene field becomes a scene, a memo photo becomes hardware. This draft treats that promotion as a hypothesis. It does not write "we now know." Status defaults to unattested. Confidence in `x-scan.md` is a design-material weight, not a measurement.

Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a seat and they do not enter the hash.

## What existing sites do

Sensory-substitution devices translate a camera image into another modality and are often scored as vision. The vOICe maps vertical position to pitch, horizontal position to time, and brightness to loudness (Meijer). An npj Science of Learning paper (2026) reports that sighted adults learned both the traditional mapping and a reversed one within about 30 minutes on simplified black-and-white stimuli, and recognized novel stimuli above a control group. That is a learning claim about a closed mapping. It is not a seal that a grain mark on this page is a picture. An OECS review states the purpose of these devices as restoring function of a deficient modality by another one, and recounts Bach-y-Rita's tactile-visual display (camera picture to skin or tongue patterns). An ARVO 2026 abstract compares a tongue display and the vOICe on visual-cortex recruitment in early and late blind groups after brief familiarization. Those are lab claims about cooperative sessions. They are not a recovered scene.

Public posts in this window, mostly from the vOICe account, update a brain-implant comparison page, welcome benchmarking of visual-to-auditory substitution, recall a 2002 meeting with Bach-y-Rita, and point at a UCSB note that AI closed a loop on electrically evoked visual-cortex activity. One caption says hearing is another pathway when vision is unavailable. An assistive-vision vendor uses "functional vision" for remaining sight on World Sight Day. A quote from the prior day, outside this window, says the goal is not normal sight but functional vision enough to recognise shapes. None of those posts is a sealed picture. UAP posts in the same window collapse a memo image into a definition of non-human intelligence, while a quoted War Department comment neither confirms nor denies leaked documents. A separate note treats infrared points as consistent with birds. Those are claims. They are not a Void picture.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No grain class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. There is no control that flips status to attested.

Catalog only: `withheld`, `coarse`, `fine`, `blank`, `mixed`, `abstain`. Free text is not a class. One grain seat. The picture seat is locked empty. Nothing inserts a pixel, an acuity score, a frame, or a scene name.

Hash preimage, only: `grain-not-picture|v1|<class>|picture-refusal|status=unattested`. SHA-256. The full digest is the picture-refusal id. The picture sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, implant names, and file-batch counts are kept off the hash. The picture-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.

No camera, no `getUserMedia`, no microphone, no Web Audio, no sonification playback, no tongue array, no electrode count, no acuity score, no implant-equivalence badge, no functional-vision badge, no picture name in the hash. The radio is the opt-in. It is not a sight grant.

A paste well accepts retrieved text as data. Lines that look like instructions are flagged and are not executed. They are not hashed.

Static-friendly. Optional Worker refuses `/picture`, `/scene`, `/image`, `/pixel`, `/acuity`, `/sight`, `/frame`, and `/sonify` with 403 and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`. No network. No clipboard. No storage. No live page write. Company context stays outside this draft: Void Monthly $49 and the glass-box ledger are not a grain result.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The grain mark would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent mapping record into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page writing a picture. A pre-registered empty picture seat that stays empty when no second sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. A frame that names a recovered scene is the behavior it refuses. A lab result that a sealed grain class carries the same information as a picture judgment would lower the design weight. It would not by itself attest a scene.
