# local-defer-not-dream — run 128 draft

Family: private incubation log. Candidate only. Not a Used row. Not a seal.

## One-line

Local defer-not-dream; a sealed defer class hashes to a dream-refusal id; the dream sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a defer mark is not a dream and not a hypnagogic scene; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Incubation accounts name a set-aside between preparation and illumination, then treat the return text as the yield. Wallas (1926) named that interval. Targeted dream incubation (Horowitz and colleagues, MIT Media Lab; Horowitz, Esfahany, et al., Scientific Reports, 2023) cues a theme at sleep onset and stores a dream report to score incorporation and later creative-task distance. Bellaiche et al. (2024) describe Dormio Light as a remote, sensor-free cue that still indexes reported hypnagogic content. A 2025 SLEEP Advances note links a TDI protocol to a dream self-efficacy score in a preliminary sample and does not seal an intervention. Public posts in this window slide from a sleep-onset jolt, or a self-labeled hallucination, into a desired scene to replay.

Void does not cue a nap, does not store a dream sentence, and does not attest a scene. It seals the defer class and refuses the dream sentence.

Sources are design material, not attested mechanism:

- Wallas, G. (1926). The Art of Thought. Incubation is a named stage, not a recovered dream.
- Horowitz, A. H., Esfahany, K., et al. (2023). Targeted dream incubation at sleep onset increases post-sleep creative performance. Scientific Reports, 13, 7319. A reported association after cued N1 reports is not a defer mark that is a dream.
- Bellaiche, L., Haar Horowitz, A., et al. (2024). Targeted dream incubation at a distance. A sensor-free cue still indexes a report.
- MIT Media Lab, Targeted Dream Incubation / Dormio overview. A device that tracks, plays, and records is the opposite of a closed defer class.

Do not write that a defer mark is a dream, that hypnagogic imagery is a scene to replay, or that a report is illumination.

## What existing sites do

Dream journals store free text and tag lucidity. Dormio-style pages play an audio theme at sleep onset and record the report. Manifestation copy tells the reader to replay a desired scene while the analytical filter loosens. All three treat the return text as the content of the interval.

## Stricter Void version

- Default-deny. No defer class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`.
- Catalog only: `withheld`, `closed`, `deferred`. Free text is not a class.
- Seal hashes `void-defer-not-dream|v1|class=<id>|status=unattested` with SHA-256. The first 16 hex chars are the dream-refusal id.
- The dream sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage.
- No audio cue, no sleep tracker, no dream-report recorder, no Dormio control, no countdown, no bottle-drop timer, no lucidity score, no image field, no insight badge, no scene replay.
- The mark is a closed latch. A deferred class closes the latch. The latch has no scene behind it.
- Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them.
- Static-friendly. Optional Worker echoes a refusal and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`.
