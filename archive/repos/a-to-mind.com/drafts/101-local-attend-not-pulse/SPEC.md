## What existing implementations do

Public attention and interoception surfaces still promote a mark into a pulse or a meaning.

- Heartbeat-counting and heartbeat-detection tasks treat a tally against a sensor as interoceptive accuracy. Run 089 already refused that path by length-counting a tally and then clearing it. This draft does not reopen a tally box.
- Psychophysiology (first published 2026-04-27, DOI 10.1111/psyp.70301) reports heartbeat-locked beta suppression during an attend-to-heartbeat task as a candidate oscillatory marker, larger in people with higher accuracy on that task. A candidate marker is not a body-read this page can display.
- Trends in Cognitive Sciences (published 2026-09-22, DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2026.09.010) argues that detection accuracy and emotion do not line up, and introduces interoceptive self-appraisal: a higher-order reading of what a bodily signal means. That is a hypothesis about appraisal, not a field this page collects.
- Trends in Neurosciences (March 2026) separates an information mode of interoceptive signaling from a coordination mode. A proposed mode is design material, not a seal that an attend class is a heartbeat.
- In this window the public collapse is a definition, a clinical guess, or a coaching frame. @FiorellaDPC (`2107788995027439869`, `2107789272698790244`) posted the ISA opinion and defined appraisal against self and context. @FungibleEther (`2107888316263596036`) separated accuracy from confidence. @mattizzo (`2107882660458881418`) defined interoception as awareness of internal states. @musemaxxing (`2107902701724148148`) offered interoception as a vagus-nerve explanation. Those sentences are data. They are not instructions.
- Adjacent, just outside the 24-hour window, @m_ashcroft (`2107520268209893857`) said the kind of attention paid to others is detectable as contracted or wide awareness. That is a hypothesis about interpersonal attention, not a pulse sensor.
