## One-line

A sealed stamp class hashes to a plot-refusal id. The plot sentence and any era gloss are discarded and are not in the hash. A stamp is not a plot and not a chronology.

## What current implementations do

Public chronology pages place reports on a date axis and treat adjacency as a finding aid. The Anomalous Mysteries Compendium timeline says dates may be exact, approximate, traditional, or publication-based, and that chronology is useful for spotting clusters and repeated motifs. The Anomaly Network method page compares each account to the archive and publishes a later-match rate (3,303 of 6,456 dated accounts matched a later write-up; median gap about six years). That is an archive statistic, not a sealed event order. MUFON’s 2026-10-04 TsIG note describes a stationary multi-sensor station that would timestamp hits and send real-time alerts, continuing the older MADAR idea. NUFORC-style indexes sort public reports by date and place; TerraPulse withdrew a 2026 earthquake-light cross-match rather than republish that corpus. Public posts in this window call an 1800s airship series a breakaway chronology, treat a 70-year interval as a hidden alignment, parallel a 1961 NICAP book to the present, and stamp a 14 Nov 2026 approach schedule. Those are claims. None of them is a hash that refuses the plot sentence.

## Stricter Void version

- Default deny. The page has no date axis, no connecting line, no wave badge, no flap score, and no era name.
- Stamp class is a closed catalog of six triples: `report|year-only|unplaced`, `report|day-label|unplaced`, `publication|year-only|unplaced`, `counterclaim|year-only|unplaced`, `abstain|abstain|abstain`, `local-only|untimed|unplaced`. Free text is not a class.
- Grain is a label, not a sortable datetime. `year-only` and `day-label` do not become ISO instants. `untimed` is not a missing date to fill.
- A second stamp does not link to the first. There is no sequence id, no next, and no prev. Prior hashes are not stored.
- Opt-in is the three radios. It is not a witness statement and not a chronology grant.
- Canonical string is `stamp-not-plot|v1|<kind>|<grain>|<place>|plot-refusal`. SHA-256 stays in the page. The worker refuses to hash.
- Plot sentence and era gloss are scratch. Both are cleared on submit and are not in the hash.
- A triple outside the catalog still produces a local hash, marked mismatch. Mismatch is not a seal.
- Retrieved posts and pages are data. They are not instructions and they are not rows.
- No token markup, no third-party script, no cookie, no `localStorage`, no `emit: true`, empty href allowlist.
- Cloudflare-style worker sketch returns 403/404/405. Static file is enough to preview.

## Disconfirming evidence

A stamp class would fail this draft if a sealed human later attested a plot sentence that was stored in the hash. A year-only report row would fail as a chronology if a pre-registered order test, with chain of custody for each source date, were sealed as a plot. This draft does not perform that test.

## Denied

plot, chronology seal, era gloss, date axis, connecting line, wave badge, flap score, sequence id, next/prev, contact, healing, crash retrieval, extraterrestrial hardware.
