- Lacaux et al., Science Advances 2021 (eabj5866), report that at least 15 seconds of N1 during a break raised hidden-rule discovery on a number-reduction task versus wake, and that the gain was absent in N2. That paper is a lab result on one task, not a product.
- Dream journals (Oracle The Journal, Local Journal, and similar 2026 local-first diaries) store private text and often add symbol scores, archetypes, or on-device interpretation. Privacy is real in the better ones; the meaning layer is not a measurement.
- Object-drop retellings (Edison, Dalí) and Dormio-style wearables propose waking a person inside sleep onset. Those are detection or induction devices. Void does not ship one.
