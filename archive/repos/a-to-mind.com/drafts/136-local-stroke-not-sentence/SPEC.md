# local-stroke-not-sentence — run 136 draft

## One-line

Local stroke-not-sentence; a sealed stroke class hashes to a sentence-refusal id; the sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a stroke mark is not a sentence and not a decoded intention; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Family: non-lexical intent capture. Design material only. A public post can treat a mark, a glossary line, or a lab caption as a sentence already decoded from intention. This draft treats that promotion as a hypothesis. It does not write "we now know." Status defaults to unattested. Confidence in `x-scan.md` is a design-material weight, not a measurement.

Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a seat and they do not enter the hash.

## What existing sites do

Speech neuroprostheses try to map cortical patterns to text. A Stanford-led Cell paper (14 Aug 2025; Kunz, Meschede-Krasa, Willett and colleagues) reported inner-speech patterns in four implanted participants, a 125,000-word training bank, sentence error on the order of 26% to 54% in one summary and accuracy up to 74% in another, and a imagined password phrase meant to keep decoding off until the phrase is thought. A Science recap of that work was dated 9 Oct 2026. Those are lab claims about cooperative, implanted sessions. They are not a seal that a stroke on this page is a sentence. This draft does not implant, does not record, and does not accept a password phrase.

Company notes are a different object. A Neuralink blog claim dated 1 Oct 2026 says more than 50,000 hours of unlabeled neural recordings were used for cursor control and cites a bits-per-second cursor record. A separate company clip claim describes audible speech from a VOICE-trial participant. Cursor motion is not a sentence. A company clip is not a hashed intention. This page stores neither.

Older AAC failure modes still matter as design material. Facilitated communication and similar assisted spelling put a mark on a board while another person is in the loop; message-passing controls have not supported the claim that the mark is the seated person's sentence. This draft has no letter board and no facilitator.

Public posts in this window do three versions. A poll lists mind reading as a superpower. A glossary line defines telepathy as supposedly receiving thoughts. A founder note and an assistant reply say brain-computer interfaces can decode some signals under specific conditions, and that covert thought access is not what the lab result is. Those interfaces can still be read as a caption. This draft does not caption.

Earlier Void drafts in this family already refused other moves: `local-nonlexical-intent-note` (mark plus optional gloss), `local-empty-lexeme-slot` (empty lexeme), `local-gloss-withheld` (gloss withheld), `local-press-not-word` (press is not a word), `local-trace-not-lexeme` (trace is not a lexeme), `local-residue-not-caption` (residue is not a caption). This draft does not reuse those slugs and does not draw their caption chip, word field, or mind-read badge.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No stroke class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. There is no control that flips status to attested.

Catalog only: `withheld`, `unparsed`, `mark-only`, `no-lexeme`, `blank-slot`, `abstain`. Free text is not a class. One stroke seat. The sentence seat is locked empty. Nothing inserts a word, a password phrase, or a vocabulary size.

Hash preimage, only: `stroke-not-sentence|v1|<class>|sentence-refusal|status=unattested`. SHA-256. The full digest is the sentence-refusal id. The sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, words, password phrases, and vocabulary sizes are kept off the hash. The sentence-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.

No microphone, no camera, no EEG, no fMRI, no implant, no letter board, no facilitator, no inner-speech decoder, no word field, no password phrase, no WPM score, no vocabulary size, no mind-read badge, no caption chip, no promote control. The radio is the opt-in. It is not a decode grant.

Static-friendly. Optional Worker refuses `/sentence`, `/decode`, `/caption`, `/speech`, `/lexeme`, and `/intent` with 403 and stores nothing. No token markup. No allowlisted outbound href. No `emit`. No network. No clipboard. No storage. No live page write.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The stroke would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent message-passing record into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page writing a sentence. A pre-registered password gate that stays closed when no phrase is supplied is a lab behavior this draft does not copy and does not need. A pre-registered empty sentence seat that stays empty when no second sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. A caption that names a decoded intention is the behavior it refuses.
