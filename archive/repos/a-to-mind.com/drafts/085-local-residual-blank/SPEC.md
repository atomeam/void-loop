## What existing implementations do

Public synchronicity surfaces treat co-presence as a principle, then leave a residual that looks like a cause.

- Jung's 1952 essay, *Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle*, names meaningful coincidence without an efficient-cause chain. Those are literature claims, not a sealed product result.
