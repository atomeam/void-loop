## What existing implementations do

Public weak-signal tools treat a faint note as a reason to act.

- Ansoff's mid-1970s weak-signal writing, restated in later early-warning frameworks, says some disruptions show up first as ambiguous clues. Those frameworks then add thresholds that trigger management discussion. The threshold is a policy choice, not a measurement.
- Horizon-scan products (Explorer Labs radar prompts, 2026-09-22; sustainability Delphi scans such as the 2026 Sustainability Science weak-signal matrix) rank signals by intensity, likelihood, impact, or centrality, then recommend a recurring scan. The rank is a workshop score.
- Forecasting benches (the Foresight Arena post in this window) ask for hundreds of resolved predictions before an edge over consensus counts. That is a resolution rule, not a license to mark an unresolved note true.
- Product posts in this window sell lead time: avalanche alerts up to 72 hours, soil stress before canopy cameras, Wi-Fi weak signals that an agent then fixes. The figure sits inside the claim.
- A long public post reads reported model geometry as a weak signal of conscious access. That reading is a hypothesis. It is not a seal.
