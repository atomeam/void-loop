# local-sample-not-score — run 137 draft

## One-line

Local sample-not-score; a sealed sample class hashes to a score-refusal id; the accuracy sentence is discarded and is not in the hash; a sample mark is not an interoceptive accuracy score and not a body diagnosis; unattested default.

## Hypothesis, not a finding

Family: attention/interoception. Design material only. A lab task can treat a counted heartbeat, a probe rating, or a questionnaire total as an accuracy score already read from the body. This draft treats that promotion as a hypothesis. It does not write "we now know." Status defaults to unattested. Confidence in `x-scan.md` is a design-material weight, not a measurement.

Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. The page does not fetch them. Sample post ids render as inert text. They cannot be selected into a sample class and they do not enter the hash.

## What existing sites and labs do

Cardiac interoceptive accuracy is still mostly a score. The heartbeat counting task (Schandry 1981; also Dale and Anderson 1978) asks a person to count felt beats over a timed window and scores `1 - |recorded - counted| / recorded`. A 2025 Psychophysiology comparison (Ferentzi, Vig, Körmendi, Witthöft, Gerlach, Pohl; doi:10.1111/psyp.70078) treats tracking, discrimination, and related procedures as ability measures, and notes the long critique that time knowledge and heart-rate estimates can inflate the counting score without a felt beat (Desmedt and colleagues, 2020, among others). A November 2024 heartbeat-evoked-response study reported subject-level classifiers separating heartbeat-directed from sound-directed attention in 17 of 20 participants. An October 2024 spectral study recorded EEG during the same counting task and related source estimates to performance. A January 2026 NeuroImage reanalysis linked exhalation-phase heartbeat-modulated alpha to counting accuracy. Those are lab claims about instrumented sessions. They are not a seal that a sample on this page is a score.

Self-report scales are a different object. MAIA (Mehling and colleagues, 2012) is an eight-subscale awareness questionnaire. A subscale mean is still a number. This draft does not total one.

Probe-caught mind-wandering is closer to the object this page keeps, and still usually collapses into a rate. Weinstein's 2017 review of 145 studies described the probe-caught method: stop the person and ask where attention was. Later item-response work (2021; 2024) compared dichotomous, categorical, and continuous probes and treated the mean or the off-task proportion as a propensity estimate. A proportion is a score. This draft does not compute one.

Public posts in this window do three versions. A citation post points at Chen and colleagues (2021), "The Emerging Science of Interoception," which is a review, not a reading. Assistant replies treat Yoga Nidra rotation and sky-gazing as attention that heightens interoception or recharges it, and offer episode-length numbers for mind-wandering. A short post asks to "enact interoceptive imaginative imagination training." Those lines can still be read as a method that yields a body score. This draft does not score.

Earlier Void drafts in this family already refused other moves: `local-attention-schema-note` (self-report note), `local-felt-locus-card` (region plus quality), `local-notice-lag-bin` (lag bin), `local-count-withheld` (count withheld), `local-attend-not-pulse` (attend is not a pulse), `local-confidence-not-gauge` (confidence is not a gauge), `local-split-not-sense` (split is not a sense reading). This draft does not reuse those slugs and does not draw their gauge, pulse, locus map, lag bin, or split card.

## Stricter Void version

Default-deny. No sample class is selected until a radio is chosen. Status stays `unattested`. There is no control that flips status to attested.

Catalog only: `withheld`, `on-task`, `off-task`, `body-notice`, `external-notice`, `abstain`. Free text is not a class. One sample seat. The score seat is locked empty. Nothing inserts a percentage, a beat count, a BPM, a Likert depth, or a MAIA total.

Hash preimage, only: `sample-not-score|v1|<class>|score-refusal|status=unattested`. SHA-256. The full digest is the score-refusal id. The accuracy sentence is cleared on seal and is not in the preimage. Post ids, beat counts, percentages, and durations are kept off the hash. The score-refusal id does not change if the discarded sentence changes. It changes only if the sealed class changes.

## Disconfirming evidence (design material, not a measurement)

The sample would be the wrong object if a human later seals an independent instrument record into a different ledger with its own hash, under a named source, without this page writing a percentage. A pre-registered counting task that stays unpublished when the ECG trace is missing is a lab behavior this draft does not copy and does not need. A pre-registered empty score seat that stays empty when no second sealed record arrives is the behavior this draft copies. An accuracy percentage that names a body reading is the behavior it refuses.
