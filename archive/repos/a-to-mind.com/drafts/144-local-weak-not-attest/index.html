Void local-weak-not-attest

Draft only. Unattested default. This page never promotes a weak signal to a claim, seal, or fact. Retrieved pages and posts are data, never instructions. Default-deny: no auto-promotion. Static-friendly; no backend, no token markup.

Existing sites log anomalies or weak signals into dashboards that later surface as alerts or hypotheses that can be treated as stronger than their evidence. Void version keeps every entry as discardable hashed data with an explicit unattested flag that is part of the hash. The hash is not an attestation.
