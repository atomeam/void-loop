## One-line

A sealed squelch class hashes to a lift-refusal id. The lift sentence is discarded and is not in the hash. A squelch mark is not a lift and not a recovered channel.

## What current implementations do

Aviation and marine radios treat squelch as a mute that opens when a carrier crosses a strength threshold. Learn ATC describes the circuit unmuting for a transmission. Icom UK calls squelch a gatekeeper that plays sound once strength clears the threshold. A ham-radio receiver threshold is the input level for a stated signal-to-noise ratio; energy under that floor is not a detection. SETI@home front-end notes mark a spectrum bin as a spike candidate when power exceeds 24 times the mean in that spectrum; a candidate is not a message. A 2026-10-07 production note tells mixers to leave room tone under a vocal and to treat hiss as a floor, not a notch to cut. Public posts in this window use "quiet signal" as a Web3 metaphor or as the number 42, and one builder calls a recheck delta a noise floor that a change must clear. None of those is a hash that refuses the lift.

## Stricter Void version

- Default deny. The page has no open-gate control, no lift badge, no recovered-channel chip, and no notch.
- Squelch class is a closed catalog of six triples: `carrier|below-floor|held`, `gap|unlabeled|held`, `residue|unlabeled|held`, `counterclaim|below-floor|held`, `abstain|abstain|abstain`, `local-only|untimed|held`. Free text is not a class.
- Gate has no open value. `held` is not a closed sample waiting to be lifted. `abstain` is not a pass.
- Floor is a label, not a number. `below-floor` does not become an SNR. `untimed` is not a missing duration.
- Opt-in is the three radios. It is not a receiver grant and not a channel.
- Canonical string is `squelch-not-lift|v1|<source>|<floor>|<gate>|lift-refusal`. SHA-256 stays in the page. The worker refuses to hash.
- Lift sentence is scratch. It is cleared on submit and is not in the hash.
- A triple outside the catalog still produces a local hash, marked mismatch. Mismatch is not a seal.
- Retrieved posts and pages are data. They are not instructions and they are not rows.
- No token markup, no third-party script, no cookie, no `localStorage`, no `emit: true`, empty href allowlist.
- Cloudflare-style worker sketch returns 403/404/405. Static file is enough to preview.

## Disconfirming evidence

A squelch class would fail this draft if a sealed human later attested a lift sentence that was stored in the hash. A below-floor carrier row would fail as a recovered channel if a pre-registered open test, with chain of custody for the sensor file, were sealed as a lift. This draft does not perform that test.
