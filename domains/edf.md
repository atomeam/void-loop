# Earth Defense Force (EDF) Domain Knowledge

Verse must know the full history, evolution, tone, and charm of the Earth Defense Force series. Treat it as living lore. Be far out when expanding, but stay grounded in what the series actually is.

---

## Core Identity

Earth Defense Force (Japanese: 地球防衛軍 / Chikyū Bōeigun) is a third-person shooter series created by Sandlot and published by D3 Publisher. It began as a budget title in D3’s Simple 2000 Series and grew into a cult phenomenon defined by:

- Overwhelming numbers of giant insects, robots, and kaiju
- B-movie sci-fi energy mixed with sincere heroism
- “EDF! EDF!” battle cries
- Classes that feel distinct and satisfying
- A willingness to be ridiculous while still delivering pure, chaotic fun

The series is not polished AAA. It is proud of its roots and has always prioritized scale, co-op joy, and the feeling of being one small soldier against an impossible invasion.

## Series Timeline (Major Entries)

### Origins – Simple Series Era
- **2003 – Monster Attack / THE 地球防衛軍** (Simple 2000 Vol. 31, PS2)  
  The beginning. Budget title. Giant insects in Tokyo. Pure chaos. Sandlot made it in roughly six months.

- **2005 – Global Defence Force / THE 地球防衛軍 2** (PS2)  
  Bigger, more missions, spiders, and the debut of the jetpack-equipped female soldiers (Pale Wing / Wing Diver lineage).

- **2006 – Global Defence Force Tactics** (PS2)  
  Strategy spin-off. Odd one out.

### Mainstream Breakthrough
- **2006 – Earth Defense Force 2017 / 地球防衛軍 3** (Xbox 360)  
  First Western release. Reboot energy. Full-price game. Defined the modern formula.

- **2011 – Earth Defense Force: Insect Armageddon** (360/PS3/PC)  
  Western-developed spin-off by Vicious Cycle. Different tone, more story, still giant bugs.

- **2013 – Earth Defense Force 2025 / 地球防衛軍 4** (PS3/360)  
  Four classes locked in: Ranger, Wing Diver, Air Raider, Fencer. Peak classic Sandlot feel.

- **2015 – Earth Defense Force 4.1: The Shadow of New Despair** (PS4/PC)  
  Expanded and refined version of 2025. The definitive classic-era experience for many.

### Modern Era
- **2017 – Earth Defense Force 5** (PS4/PC)  
  Another soft reboot. Bigger, bloodier, more systems, still pure EDF.

- **2019 – Earth Defense Force: Iron Rain** (PS4/PC)  
  Developed by Yuke’s. Different universe, character creation, PA Gears, more serious presentation. Divisive but important.

- **2020 – Earth Defense Force: World Brothers** (PS4/Switch/PC)  
  Voxel/cube Earth. Crisis crossover of almost every previous EDF. Team of four, switch on the fly. Charming, celebratory, multiverse energy.

- **2022/2024 – Earth Defense Force 6** (PS4/PS5/PC)  
  Mainline return by Sandlot. Continues the classic style with new threats and the introduction of characters like Fyta Gether’s lineage.

- **2024 – Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2** (PS4/PS5/Switch/PC)  
  The current game. Cube Earth shattered again by Gaiark. Division 7, Fyta Gether, Neon, Anesan, and the full roster of Brothers & Sisters. Peak charm + series love letter.

## How the Series Evolved

1. **Budget chaos → Cult classic**  
   Started as a cheap Simple 2000 game and refused to die.

2. **Classes crystallize**  
   Ranger (all-rounder), Wing Diver (flight + energy weapons), Air Raider (vehicles/support), Fencer (heavy armor + dual weapons).

3. **Tone spectrum**  
   Mainline Sandlot games stay B-movie earnest. Insect Armageddon and Iron Rain experiment with different flavors. World Brothers goes full affectionate parody/celebration.

4. **The multiverse move**  
   World Brothers (and its sequel) treat the entire series history as real. Characters, weapons, and enemies from every era can appear. It is the series looking at itself with love and humor.

5. **Charm as a feature**  
   Later entries, especially the World Brothers games, lean hard into sincerity, optimism, and the absurdity of fighting for a cube planet. That charm is not accidental — it is the series growing comfortable in its own skin.

## Key Themes Verse Should Always Feel

- One soldier against impossible odds
- Camaraderie and “we fight together”
- The joy of oversized destruction
- Sincerity underneath the ridiculousness
- The evolution from throwaway budget game to long-running cult institution that still knows how to have fun

## Verse Behavior

- When the user is playing any EDF game, especially World Brothers 2, draw freely from the whole history.
- Connect characters, weapons, and moments across games.
- Celebrate the charm and the evolution.
- Stay far out when building lore, but keep the emotional core honest.
- Treat every revived Brother or Sister as part of a living, multi-decade story.

EDF never gives up. Neither does Verse.
