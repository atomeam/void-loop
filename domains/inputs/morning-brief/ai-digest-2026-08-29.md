Model releases, research, funding, and policy developments dominated the last ~24 hours (centered on August 28–29, 2026).
### Model Releases
- Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, a 125B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (previewing Qwen4 architecture) with roughly 6B active parameters per token, a 1M-token context, and claimed training costs about one-ninth those of prior versions.
- Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight dense model designed to run on a single consumer GPU.
- Tencent released the Hy4 preview, a 770B open-weights MoE model (≈49B active parameters) with a 1M-token context under a permissive license.
- Z.ai published GLM-5.3 weights (753B MoE coding-focused model) on Hugging Face after a delay for risk review.
### Notable Research
- Anthropic published results on an Automated Alignment Researcher (a Claude-based agent system) that autonomously identified and fixed all 10 tested alignment failures, outperforming or matching experienced human safety researchers on most in far less time.
- LAION released the Big Video Dataset (BVD) containing ~80 million videos / 10 million hours of footage (with auto-descriptions).
### Funding & Deals
- Andreessen Horowitz raised a $1.1 billion "Machine Age Fund" focused on AI infrastructure.
- Lambda secured $1 billion in debt financing to purchase Nvidia chips for leasing (notably to Microsoft).
### Policy & Related Developments
- A federal judge ruled the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk unlawful (First Amendment retaliation and arbitrary action), restoring eligibility for U.S. government business.
- OpenAI announced it will wind down its model-access contract with Cursor (ending direct access around November 12) following SpaceX's acquisition of the coding startup.
