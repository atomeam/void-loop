# A-to-Mind

> Void: a blank stage with one quiet input. Ask for something and it appears. Nothing is shown until someone asks.

## What you can ask for today
- A clock, timer, counter, sticky note, notepad, list, calculator, shape, image or link card ("make a 5 minute timer", "draw a circle", "add a sticky that says hi").
- Changes to what's on the stage ("make it blue", "make everything bigger", "clear").
- A page about almost anything ("what is a black hole", "who was Ada Lovelace"): a floating summary drawn from Wikipedia, with its source linked.
- "what can you do" shows Void's current skills.

## Notes for crawlers and agents
- Everything happens in the browser at https://a-to-mind.com. There is no public API, CLI or SDK.
- Earlier pages on this domain described a different product (workflow execution, treaties, ledgers). They are retired.
