## One-liner

Service that turns unused personal objects into storied artifacts with generated histories, then preserves them as digital heirlooms **or** sells enhanced versions. Physical-artifact analog to Ghost Finishing: emotional friction / personal residue → transactional provenance.

## Thesis / problem

Attic and closet objects — a chipped teapot, a ticket stub, an unused wedding gift, a forgotten tool — carry faint personal meaning and low market value. RelicWeaver extracts that residue, weaves a coherent narrative that is partly invented and partly researched, and attaches the story so the object gains emotional or commercial value.

## How (mechanics sketch — no architecture)

Photo + scraps of context → visual analysis of wear / materials / style + public historical / cultural data + optional family details → short illustrated "life of the object" (agency / biography) → digital booklet, optional AR overlay, printable labels / certificates. Keep as private heirloom **or** list on a curated marketplace; owner splits proceeds.

Startup shape: vision + LLM + small editor team for fact-check / polish. Moat: personal context + object storytelling + marketplace that treats narrative as product.

### Thesis

Sell **last-known-location memory kits** for objects people lose forever — childhood bike left when the house sold, jacket on a foreign train, coffee mug from an old job. The object is gone; the feeling remains.

Not nostalgia merch. A portable tactile **last known location** of something that no longer exists. Cheaper than therapy, more concrete than a photo.

### How it works (three steps)

1. **Customer describes** the lost object in sensory detail — weight, smell, rattle, worn spot where the hand rested.
2. **Maker network reconstructs** a physical facsimile that is **not** a replica of the original, but a reconstruction of the **last moment they remember holding it** — same weight, texture where the hand rested, same small damage.
3. **Ship with short audio** of the ambient sound probably playing the last time they had it — bus route, kitchen radio that year, rain on that roof.

### Price band

**$180–$450** made-to-order kit (facsimile + last-sonic-environment audio).

### Gap (why this is distinct)

Memory businesses sell photos, letters, and generic keepsake boxes. Nobody systematically manufactures **physical residue of a specific lost object** + its **last sonic environment**. RelicWeaver core works on objects that *still exist*; Last-Known-Location works on objects that are *gone*.
