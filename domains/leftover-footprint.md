## One-liner

Rebuild a vanished contemporaneous placement or decision record from **aerials, invoices, interviews and third-party files** so a PE or counsel can certify it **without inventing the original log**.

## Pattern

An event already happened; the contemporaneous record is gone; the gatekeeper accepts a **dated reconstruction assembled from leftovers and labelled as reconstruction**, and rejects anything that looks **backdated or invented**.

## Gatekeeper

**EPA** (**FDEP** where the state program applies) rejects a FER that **skips required elements, hides gaps, or presents reconstructed facts as original**. 257.75(c)(1)(xii)–(xiv) requires a **narrative of data reviewed**, the **supporting leftovers listed**, and a **gap plan for Part 2**. A **PE certifies**.

## Packet

1. Cause-of-gap memo.
2. Leftover inventory with provenance.
3. Crosswalk from leftover to FER field.
4. Labelled reconstruction pages.
5. Dated interview abstracts.
6. Data-gap and Part 2 field plan.
7. PE-ready certification block.
8. Operating-record and website posting checklist.
