## One-liner

Turn an offshore ship's crew-change stops, bunker notes, and charter chain into **one emissions file a verifier accepts**, splitting **UK ETS from EU ETS** so the same tonne isn't paid twice, before those ships enter both schemes.

## The clock (per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- Offshore ships of **5,000 GT and above enter the UK ETS on 1 Jan 2027** (UK ETS Authority main response, GOV.UK, 25 Nov 2025; delayed to match the EU).
- GOV.UK guidance "UK Emissions Trading Scheme for maritime: how to comply" excludes offshore ships **only up to 31 Dec 2026**.
- The same ships **enter the EU ETS on 1 Jan 2027** (DNV 8 Nov 2024; ABS Regulatory News Feb 2025; Norwegian Environment Agency).
- EU company report for 2027 emissions due **31 Mar 2028**; surrender due **30 Sep 2028** (ABS).
- An EPRS briefing (16 Sep 2026) describes a Commission proposal to move some smaller ships later; IMCA (28 Jul 2026) says it isn't adopted and ships over 5,000 GT stay on Jan 2027.
- How many ships trade both UK and EU waters: **unverified**.

## Messy input

- Noon and dynamic-positioning (DP) logs that don't say **passage vs worksite**.
- Bunker delivery notes **without methane slip**.
- AIS showing a crew boat alongside rather than a port.
- Charter parties silent on who is the **"maritime operator"** when the ISM company changes mid-year.

## Gatekeeper

- **UK:** the UK regulator via the **METS** system and the **UK Emissions Trading Registry**. No separate Document of Compliance; **the submission is the file**.
- **EU:** an **accredited MRV verifier**, then the administering authority.
- Files are rejected for:
  - a **missing crew-relief stop** (it counts as a port of call for offshore ships, per ABS citing Directive (EU) 2023/959);
  - a **missing methane slip** (Norwegian Environment Agency);
  - a **tonne claimed in full under both schemes**.
- Penalties: UK civil penalty figure not cited. EU: **EUR 100 per tCO2e**, inflation-adjusted, allowances still due, and the **name published** (EMSA FAQ).

## Packet

1. **Monitoring-plan addendum** — worksite, crew-relief call, UK vs EU voyage.
2. **Voyage table** for CO2 / CH4 / N2O with fuel evidence.
3. **One-page operator-by-day note** with written delegation.
4. **Crosswalk** so UK domestic, NI-GB (50% UK surrender deduction), intra-EEA, and half-counted extra-EEA legs aren't stacked.
5. **Drafts** for METS and the EU system.

## Who signs

- The **UK operator signs the METS submission** (preparing it isn't a reserved licence).
- The **EU report is verified by an MRV-accredited body**. We assemble under that verifier and **don't verify**.

## New to the company (not in other ventures yet)

- The **same fuel tonne can be UK-only, EU-only, both, or neither** depending on worksite, crew change, or passage.
- The **operator can flip when the ISM company changes** mid-year.

## Rescue and prevention

- **Rescue:** fix the monitoring method before 1 Jan 2027.
- **Prevention:** a running quarterly file.

## Risks

- Class societies (DNV, LR, ABS) brief owners for free and could ship a template.
- Unclear worksite definitions could stop verifiers signing **any** third-party crosswalk.
- The dual-scheme ship count may be too small.
