## One-liner

Palimpsest marketplace for abandoned personal work: monetize the guilt between ambition and follow-through; finishers complete half-done pieces *in the original person's voice, taste, and constraints*; the unfinished marks and time-gap stay part of the value. AI first-pass + human taste/ethics. Not Fiverr/Upwork ghostwriting. Not Loose Ends (see contrast).

## Why the model works

### The Guilt Economy

Monetize the friction between a creator's ambition and their follow-through. Most people already have at least one abandoned project they feel mild guilt about — the marketplace turns that guilt into a transaction instead of another New Year's resolution.

### The Palimpsest Aesthetic

Sell the gap in time and the original messy constraints as the **core value proposition** (not a flaw to erase). Unfinished marks, original notes, the multi-year pause, the first owner's taste — visible or documented. The finished object is a palimpsest, not a clean replacement. That scarcity and story is what buyers pay for. Completely separates this from standard Fiverr ghostwriting or Upwork commissions.

### Landscape contrast — Loose Ends

Closest existing equivalent: volunteer network **Loose Ends** (looseends.org) — matches crafters to finish knitting/sewing projects of people who have **died or become disabled**.  

Ghost Finishing is the commercial counterpart for the **living**, spanning more than craft (codebases, furniture, literature, song, painting, family recipe books). Uncrowded blue ocean relative to that volunteer niche and to generic gig marketplaces.

## Loop ambition (private Books only)

Own the unfinished layer of every medium; finish better while leaving their hand visible. Face stays blank.

## Unit of value

**One scoped, style-faithful completion** (fixed scope + fixed price + done) that preserves the abandoned state as story. Not open-ended retainers.

## What gets listed (examples)

- Novel stalled at chapter 14  
- Half-built dining table  
- Unfinished song  
- Painting that sat for three years  
- Side-project codebase  
- Family recipe book that never got photos  

Finishers bid to complete in the owner's voice. Original creator keeps primary credit (or co-credit by agreement).

## Model (sketch only — no architecture)

1. Owner lists stalled artifact + voice / taste / constraints ("what must stay").
2. Finisher scopes one-shot completion (criteria + price + deadline + fidelity rules).
3. Simple hold / escrow until acceptance; ship → close. No ongoing agency by default.
4. Optional later sale / license / exhibit → proceed split per agreement.

Service buyers: makers who stalled and want closure. Finishers: restoration / ghost / style-faithful completers. Later object buyers: collectors / licensees paying for the palimpsest story; companies buying style-consistent completions as training examples or limited editions.

## Start wedge

**One medium only** at first — writing *or* furniture (pick before any build). Escrow + style-fidelity. Platform fee + optional sale/proceeds split. Simple listing + a handful of finishers who already do restoration or ghost work. Narrow enough not to be crowded; odd enough that the first finished pieces get talked about. Other media (code, song, painting, recipes) wait until one wedge has a real completion.

## Do not throw out (katamari residue → next product surface)

Guilt listings, half-files, "I don't want credit," failed bids, style mismatches — keep them. They become inventory and design signal for the next completion/residue product, not discarded noise.
