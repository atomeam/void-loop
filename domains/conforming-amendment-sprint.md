## One-liner

An accepted retirement plan document is now wrong because operations already turned on optional **SECURE / SECURE 2.0** features; the cure is a **discretionary plan amendment adopted inside a short window**.

## Pattern

The gatekeeper **already accepted the file**; the facts no longer match; the cure is a **different amendment instrument adopted inside a named window**, not a resubmission.

**Recurs in (scout automation; per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok; national counts unverified):**
- IRS-qualified plans — conforming amendment due **31 Dec 2026** for most non-governmental, non-collectively-bargained plans (IRS Employee Plans News 16 Sep 2026, restating Notice 2024-2).
- SEC **Form D** amendment "as soon as practicable" after a material mistake (SEC Form D FAQs, reviewed 8 Jul 2026).
- **Form 941** corrected on **941-X** with an interest-free window (IRS 941-X instructions).
- Florida **DR-15** replaced by a full amended return via DOR e-Services.
