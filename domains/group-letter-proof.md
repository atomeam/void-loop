## One-liner

Central orgs under an old IRS group exemption letter must prove they still qualify under the 2026 rules **before 22 Jan 2027**, or the letter dies and every chapter is treated as unfiled.

## Pattern

The customer believes they're **already out of scope**. The gatekeeper won't accept "we have always been exempt" and demands proof: **affiliation, supervision or control, one letter, the same Code paragraph, and a live subordinate list**.

**Recurs in (scout automation; per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok):**
- IRS group letters under **Rev. Proc. 2026-8**.
- State sales-tax exemption certificates that must be re-proven (**Florida DR-14 every five years**; Harbor Compliance lists **11 states** requiring renewal).
- Florida institutional, charitable, and religious **property-tax exemptions** (annual application by **1 March**, Fla. Stat. 196.011).
- **NRC** small-entity fee reduction on **Form 526**.
- Florida **CIE** religious-institution annual sworn affidavit.

The national count of group letters: **unverified**.

## Beachhead

**Central Florida** associations, denominational district offices, grand lodges, and multi-site ministries holding a group letter plus a Florida DR-14 or a Polk institutional exemption. Reachable by document intake, video walkthroughs, and an existing CPA or tax-exempt counsel filing. **No license, office, or phone needed.** (Shares the Lakeland / Polk footprint.)

## The clock (scout automation; per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- **Transition ends 22 Jan 2027** (Rev. Proc. 2026-8, IRB 2026-04; IRS "Group exemption rulings and group returns" page).
- Secondary clock: **1 Mar 2027** for Florida institutional applications (then yearly).

## Messy input

- Decades-old determination letter.
- Annotated chapter directories.
- Mixed 501(c) paragraphs; shared EINs.
- Bylaws silent on the central body.
- Missing subordinate authorizations.
- Stale DR-14.
- Property-appraiser files with the wrong legal name.

## Gatekeeper

- **IRS TE/GE** rejects, after the transition: zero subordinates; more than one letter; mixed Code paragraphs; or no documented affiliation and supervision / control.
- **Florida DOR** won't reissue a DR-14 without federal status or religious-use proof.
- A missed **1 March** county filing **waives that tax year**.

## Packet

1. Inventory of letters and EINs.
2. Subordinate roster.
3. Affiliation-and-control exhibit.
4. Subordinate authorizations.
5. One-letter consolidation memo.
6. Form 15644 / SGRI or church exception memo.
7. Form 8940 Schedule Q attachments if restating.
8. DR-5 support.
9. County exemption exhibits.

## Rescue and prevention

- **Rescue:** rebuild the roster and control story before 22 Jan 2027.
- **Prevention:** calendar the annual SGRI window (30 to 90 days before year-end) and 1 March so the pack is refreshed, not rebuilt.

## Risks

- Many church central orgs are **excepted from annual SGRI**, so the big clock is the one-time transition.
- Some holders **don't know they have a group letter**.
- Partner calendars fill in **Q4 2026**.
