## One-liner

Turn option, pre-emption, conditional-contract, and promotion agreements over registered land in England and Wales into the information pack a regulated conveyancer files with HM Land Registry.

## The clock (per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- **The Provision of Information (Contractual Control) (Registered Land) Regulations 2026**, made **8 June 2026** (Penningtons, 15 June 2026).
- **HMLR news story** 12 June 2026, updated 8 July 2026:
  - Rights granted **on or after 8 June 2026 and before 6 April 2027** must be filed **by 6 October 2027**, once the digital service opens.
  - **From 6 April 2027:** new rights, changes, and assignments must be filed **within 60 days**.
- The HMLR practice guide is still "coming months."
- **Agreements signed now are in scope, with nowhere to file yet.**

## Messy input

Signed options, promotion agreements, conditional contracts, side letters, partial red-line plans, company names that don't match registers, assignments sitting in email.

## Required fields (per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- Agreement date and title
- Party names
- Company number and country of incorporation
- Individual grantor's date and place of birth
- Right type
- Exercise conditions
- Initial and total control periods
- Any right to end
- Title numbers
- Part-of-title location data
- Address, postcode, UPRN
- Airspace or sub-surface

**Price is not filed.** Birth details and the conveyancer's registration number are **not published**.

## Gatekeeper

- **HM Land Registry (HMLR)**, reached **only via a regulated conveyancer**.
- The registrar may refuse to register or update a notice or restriction if the duty looks unmet (**reg 10 as laid**).
- Failure without reasonable excuse, or giving false information, is a **criminal offence under s.225 Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023** (Mayer Brown, 8 June 2026: up to **2 years** and / or an **unlimited fine**).
- Likely failures: missing identifier; plan not matching the title; right actually exempt.

## Exemptions as laid (per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- Control under **18 months**.
- Non-development use only.
- Security rights.
- s.106 infrastructure-only rights.

Greenberg Traurig (16 June 2026): catches control over **18 months** over a **freehold** or a **lease over 15 years**.

## Packet per agreement

1. **In-scope / exempt worksheet** for the conveyancer to confirm (we draft; they decide).
2. Party schedule.
3. Title numbers, address, part-of-title extent.
4. Right type, conditions, periods, break right.
5. Trigger date.
6. Source index.
7. **No price, no deed.**
8. Cover note listing **what we did not decide**.

## Who signs

- The **grantee owes the duty** (MHCLG guidance, 9 March 2026).
- **Only an authorised conveyancer submits**, with regulator and registration number.
- We work **under that conveyancer from the first file**. We **never lodge** and **never advise on scope**.
