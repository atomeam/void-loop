## What is now known

- **Senolytic D+Q in fibrotic MASH (fresh primary result).** Koning, Meijnikman, Kirkland, Tchkonia et al. report a phase-2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled proof-of-principle trial of intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin in fibrotic MASH ([Nature Metabolism, published 1 Oct 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01643-4); ClinicalTrials.gov [NCT05506488](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05506488)). Thirty-one participants received D+Q (dasatinib 100 mg/day + quercetin 1,000 mg/day) or placebo for three consecutive days per week over 3 weeks, repeated across three 7-week cycles (21 weeks total). Primary endpoint ≥1-stage fibrosis improvement without MASH worsening on paired biopsies: **47% D+Q vs 7% placebo (P = 0.02)**. MASH resolution 53% vs 7% (P = 0.02). snRNA-seq showed decreased senescence (SenMayo, SHGS) and fibrosis gene signatures and fewer fibrogenic / immune cell populations in the D+Q arm. Adverse events were more frequent with D+Q (82% vs 43%) and were self-limiting. Authors and sensitivity analyses treat the histology signal as **hypothesis-generating** (small n, wide CI, missing-biopsy sensitivity). This is the first human RCT evidence that intermittent senolytics can move biopsy-proven liver fibrosis in MASH.
- **Intermittent low-dose rapamycin safety (PEARL).** Influence of rapamycin on safety and healthspan metrics after one year: PEARL trial results, *Aging*, published **4 Apr 2025**, DOI [10.18632/aging.206235](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206235) (PMC12074816; NCT04488601). Forty-eight-week decentralized, double-blind RCT of weekly compounded rapamycin (5 mg or 10 mg) vs placebo in healthy normative-aging adults. Adverse and serious adverse events similar across arms; visceral adiposity (primary) unchanged; blood biomarkers stayed in normal ranges. Women on 10 mg gained lean tissue mass and reported less pain; some well-being gains at 5 mg. Epigenetic age subset (n = 24) showed no meaningful between-group change. Longest published human rapamycin healthspan RCT to date; safety is the established claim, not lifespan extension.
- **Semaglutide and epigenetic aging (post-hoc RCT).** Corley, McComsey et al., [Nature Communications, published 19 May 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72861-3): post-hoc DNAm analysis of a 32-week phase-2b RCT of once-weekly semaglutide in HIV-associated lipohypertrophy (parent NCT04019197; semaglutide n = 45, placebo n = 39 with paired methylomes). Adjusted models: slower epigenetic aging vs placebo on PhenoAge (-4.9 years/year, p = 0.004), PCGrimAge (-3.1, p = 0.007), GrimAge V2 (-2.3, p = 0.009), OMICmAge (-2.2, p = 0.009), RetroAge (-2.2, p = 0.030), DunedinPACE (-0.09 units, ~9% slower, p = 0.01), plus multi-system clocks (inflammation, brain, heart). Authors stress post-hoc design, HIV-specific cohort, 32-week follow-up, and need for prospective gerotherapeutic trials. Positions a licensed GLP-1 agonist as a **candidate** gerotherapeutic, not a proven longevity drug.
- **Live registry path (answer to open question 2).** The ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 (version 2.0.5, data timestamp 2026-10-07T09:00) returns JSON with `access-control-allow-origin: *`. That means a browser page can query trial status, enrollment, and primary-completion dates directly, with no server, by calling `https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.intr=...` or `/studies/<NCT id>` (box check 2026-10-07). PubMed E-utilities and GEO serve the paper and methylome side: the semaglutide methylome deposit is public as [GSE327270](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE327270), released 2026-04-17 (PBMC DNA methylation, platform GPL33022).

[NCT05506488](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05506488) is COMPLETED with primary completion on 2026-02-26 and `hasResults: false`. - **Senescent-cell burden predicts who responds to D+Q.** Farr, Khosla, Kirkland et al. ran a phase 2 RCT in 60 postmenopausal women ([Nature Medicine 30:2605, 2 Jul 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03096-2); NCT04313634). The primary endpoint (CTx bone resorption at 20 weeks) missed (P = 0.611). In the highest tertile of T-cell p16 expression, D+Q raised P1NP by 34%, lowered CTx by 11%, and raised radius BMD by 2.7% (P = 0.004), all exploratory. Follow-up work ([Aging Cell 24:e14489, 17 Jan 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14489)) shows the p16 *variant 5* transcript predicts response better than variant 1+5 and rises only after senescent cells pass a threshold. A correlated plasma SASP panel works as an easier substitute. This is the mechanism a replication trial in MASH should use to pick participants, so a second trial can test the people most likely to respond.
- **Field-level status (fresh review).** Trivedi et al., PRISMA systematic review of human senolytic, senomorphic and mTOR trials ([Br J Clin Pharmacol, online 5 Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70871)): six completed studies qualified. D+Q showed preliminary functional and biomarker gains with mild adverse events, and the authors classify senolytics as investigational and call for larger trials with standardised senescence biomarkers. This matches the hypothesis-generating framing above.

- **A blood methylation measure of senescence burden (preprint).** Simpson, Crofts, Mavrommatis, White, ... LeBrasseur, Chandra, "Methylation-associated Gene Expression (MaGE): a non-invasive, in vivo measure of cellular senescence", [medRxiv, posted 1 Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.29.26364335) (PMID 42856468; not yet peer reviewed). Elastic-net predictors trained on paired blood methylation (EPIC v1) and p16INK4a, p14ARF and p21CIP1 expression in the Mayo MOA-2 cohort, then applied to 18,859 Generation Scotland volunteers. Findings: 45 Bonferroni-significant associations across 18 incident diseases and all-cause mortality; MaGE-p16 has the largest mortality association (HR 1.38 per SD, 95% CI 1.28-1.49, P = 2.7e-17, 842 deaths over 10 years). MaGE also tracked COVID-19 severity (GSE167202) and Crohn's resolution after treatment (paired pediatric samples). The authors name stratification in senolytic trials as the use. This matters because a methylation score can be computed on blood already banked by any trial that ran an array, so burden can be tested after the fact.
