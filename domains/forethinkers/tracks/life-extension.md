## What is now known

- **Senolytic D+Q in fibrotic MASH (fresh primary result).** Koning, Meijnikman, Kirkland, Tchkonia et al. report a phase-2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled proof-of-principle trial of intermittent dasatinib plus quercetin in fibrotic MASH ([Nature Metabolism, published 1 Oct 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01643-4); ClinicalTrials.gov [NCT05506488](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05506488)). Thirty-one participants received D+Q (dasatinib 100 mg/day + quercetin 1,000 mg/day) or placebo for three consecutive days per week over 3 weeks, repeated across three 7-week cycles (21 weeks total). Primary endpoint ≥1-stage fibrosis improvement without MASH worsening on paired biopsies: **47% D+Q vs 7% placebo (P = 0.02)**. MASH resolution 53% vs 7% (P = 0.02). snRNA-seq showed decreased senescence (SenMayo, SHGS) and fibrosis gene signatures and fewer fibrogenic / immune cell populations in the D+Q arm. Adverse events were more frequent with D+Q (82% vs 43%) and were self-limiting. Authors and sensitivity analyses treat the histology signal as **hypothesis-generating** (small n, wide CI, missing-biopsy sensitivity). This is the first human RCT evidence that intermittent senolytics can move biopsy-proven liver fibrosis in MASH.
- **Intermittent low-dose rapamycin safety (PEARL).** Influence of rapamycin on safety and healthspan metrics after one year: PEARL trial results, *Aging*, published **4 Apr 2025**, DOI [10.18632/aging.206235](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206235) (PMC12074816; NCT04488601). Forty-eight-week decentralized, double-blind RCT of weekly compounded rapamycin (5 mg or 10 mg) vs placebo in healthy normative-aging adults. Adverse and serious adverse events similar across arms; visceral adiposity (primary) unchanged; blood biomarkers stayed in normal ranges. Women on 10 mg gained lean tissue mass and reported less pain; some well-being gains at 5 mg. Epigenetic age subset (n = 24) showed no meaningful between-group change. Longest published human rapamycin healthspan RCT to date; safety is the established claim, not lifespan extension.
- **Semaglutide and epigenetic aging (post-hoc RCT).** Corley, McComsey et al., [Nature Communications, published 19 May 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72861-3): post-hoc DNAm analysis of a 32-week phase-2b RCT of once-weekly semaglutide in HIV-associated lipohypertrophy (parent NCT04019197; semaglutide n = 45, placebo n = 39 with paired methylomes). Adjusted models: slower epigenetic aging vs placebo on PhenoAge (-4.9 years/year, p = 0.004), PCGrimAge (-3.1, p = 0.007), GrimAge V2 (-2.3, p = 0.009), OMICmAge (-2.2, p = 0.009), RetroAge (-2.2, p = 0.030), DunedinPACE (-0.09 units, ~9% slower, p = 0.01), plus multi-system clocks (inflammation, brain, heart). Authors stress post-hoc design, HIV-specific cohort, 32-week follow-up, and need for prospective gerotherapeutic trials. Positions a licensed GLP-1 agonist as a **candidate** gerotherapeutic, not a proven longevity drug.
- **Live registry path (answer to open question 2).** The ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 (version 2.0.5, data timestamp 2026-10-07T09:00) returns JSON with `access-control-allow-origin: *`. That means a browser page can query trial status, enrollment, and primary-completion dates directly, with no server, by calling `https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.intr=...` or `/studies/<NCT id>` (box check 2026-10-07). PubMed E-utilities and GEO serve the paper and methylome side: the semaglutide methylome deposit is public as [GSE327270](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE327270), released 2026-04-17 (PBMC DNA methylation, platform GPL33022).
- **No senolytic MASH replication is registered yet (2026-10-07).** A search of senolytic, D+Q, fisetin, navitoclax, and senescence terms under MASH/NASH/steatohepatitis conditions returned NCT05506488 as the only senolytic interventional trial (79 hits in total, the rest off-target). [NCT05506488](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05506488) is COMPLETED with primary completion on 2026-02-26 and `hasResults: false`. Its actual enrollment is listed as 30, while the 2026-10-03 note recorded 31 participants from the paper, so the card should cite the paper's analysed count and link the registry. Of the 22 D+Q trials in the registry, the nearest metabolic or systemic relatives are [NCT07144293](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07144293) (D+Q for frailty in HIV, phase 2, 82 enrolled, completed 2026-08-26, results not yet posted) and [NCT05653258](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05653258) (adipose snRNA-seq senescence map, phase 2/3, 160, primary completion 2027-02).
- **Senescent-cell burden predicts who responds to D+Q.** Farr, Khosla, Kirkland et al. ran a phase 2 RCT in 60 postmenopausal women ([Nature Medicine 30:2605, 2 Jul 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03096-2); NCT04313634). The primary endpoint (CTx bone resorption at 20 weeks) missed (P = 0.611). In the highest tertile of T-cell p16 expression, D+Q raised P1NP by 34%, lowered CTx by 11%, and raised radius BMD by 2.7% (P = 0.004), all exploratory. Follow-up work ([Aging Cell 24:e14489, 17 Jan 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14489)) shows the p16 *variant 5* transcript predicts response better than variant 1+5 and rises only after senescent cells pass a threshold. A correlated plasma SASP panel works as an easier substitute. This is the mechanism a replication trial in MASH should use to pick participants, so a second trial can test the people most likely to respond.
- **Field-level status (fresh review).** Trivedi et al., PRISMA systematic review of human senolytic, senomorphic and mTOR trials ([Br J Clin Pharmacol, online 5 Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70871)): six completed studies qualified. D+Q showed preliminary functional and biomarker gains with mild adverse events, and the authors classify senolytics as investigational and call for larger trials with standardised senescence biomarkers. This matches the hypothesis-generating framing above.
- **GLP-1 epigenetic slowing is now under prospective test.** Three registered trials take DNA-methylation age as a primary outcome:
  - [NCT07220473](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07220473), "Tirzepatide to Slow Biological Aging". UTMB Galveston, randomized double-blind phase 2, 90 participants, started 2026-02-27, primary completion 2027-11. Primary outcome is the change in DNAm biological age at weeks 4, 12 and 24.
  - [NCT07707778](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07707778), tirzepatide and epigenetic aging in virally suppressed people with HIV. National Taiwan University, non-randomized phase 4, 60 participants, started 2026-06-25, primary completion 2027-12. Primary outcomes are second- and third-generation clocks at weeks 33 and 57. This is the closest population match to the semaglutide HIV post-hoc result.
  - [NCT07293325](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07293325), semaglutide versus tirzepatide. Zhejiang University, randomized open-label, 66 participants, primary completion 2027-04-02. Primary outcome is the iWatchAge DNAm age at 24 weeks.
- **Rapamycin healthspan trials in the pipeline (2026-10-07 registry).** Thirteen active mTOR-inhibitor aging trials, including [NCT06727305](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06727305) (mTOR inhibitors in older adults, phase 1/2, 60, primary completion 2027-09), [NCT06658093](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06658093) (RESTOR PK/PD, 194, primary completion 2028-07), and [NCT07191353](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07191353) (rapamycin plus resistance training for bone, phase 2, 148, primary completion 2027-03-01). These extend PEARL's safety signal toward functional endpoints.
- **Burden-selected senolytic trials, full registry pass (2026-10-14).** Queried ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 at 2026-10-14 18:14 UTC for dasatinib with quercetin (22 records), fisetin (37), "senolytic" (31), navitoclax (45), UBX0101/UBX1325/foselutoclax (8) and procyanidin C1 (1): 115 unique records. Eligibility and primary-outcome text were searched for p16, SASP, senescence and burden. Eight records mention senescence in eligibility; seven of those only exclude drugs that cause senescence (chemotherapy). One enriches by burden:
  - [NCT07270120](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07270120), "Senolytics to Improve Physical and Cognitive Function in Older Adults With Multiple Sclerosis". Ohio State University (PI Yinan Zhang), phase 1, single arm, open label, 30 participants aged 50-85 with secondary progressive MS. Exclusion 21 removes anyone with p16INK4a below the median of the OSU Aging and MS Cohort, so everyone treated has above-median burden. Dosing is dasatinib 100 mg plus quercetin 1,250 mg on 2 days every 2 weeks for 12 weeks. Primary outcomes are retention, recruitment feasibility and adverse effects. Status NOT_YET_RECRUITING; estimated start 2026-01-15 and primary completion 2027-01-15, with the record last updated 2025-12-08.
  - The answer for today: burden selection has reached the registry once, as a feasibility study with no placebo arm. A randomized, burden-enriched efficacy trial is the missing design, and the Mayo bone result (Farr 2024) remains the strongest human evidence that it would help.
- **A blood methylation measure of senescence burden (preprint).** Simpson, Crofts, Mavrommatis, White, ... LeBrasseur, Chandra, "Methylation-associated Gene Expression (MaGE): a non-invasive, in vivo measure of cellular senescence", [medRxiv, posted 1 Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.29.26364335) (PMID 42856468; not yet peer reviewed). Elastic-net predictors trained on paired blood methylation (EPIC v1) and p16INK4a, p14ARF and p21CIP1 expression in the Mayo MOA-2 cohort, then applied to 18,859 Generation Scotland volunteers. Findings: 45 Bonferroni-significant associations across 18 incident diseases and all-cause mortality; MaGE-p16 has the largest mortality association (HR 1.38 per SD, 95% CI 1.28-1.49, P = 2.7e-17, 842 deaths over 10 years). Supplement 5 (basic models): MaGE-p16 with interstitial pulmonary fibrosis HR 2.11 (1.50-2.97, 42 cases) and MaGE-p21 with alcoholic liver disease HR 2.65 (1.69-4.14, 30 cases), matching where each marker is known to act. MaGE also tracked COVID-19 severity (GSE167202) and Crohn's resolution after treatment (paired pediatric samples). The authors name stratification in senolytic trials as the use. This matters because a methylation score can be computed on blood already banked by any trial that ran an array, so burden can be tested after the fact.
  - Availability, checked 2026-10-14: analysis code is public ([GitHub dsimps1993/Methylation-associated-Gene-Expression-Predictors](https://github.com/dsimps1993/Methylation-associated-Gene-Expression-Predictors), pushed 2026-09-28, no licence file), but the CpG coefficient files it loads (`p16Ink4a_tcellrna_coefficients.csv` and siblings) sit on the lab's own storage and are not in the repo or the eight medRxiv supplements. Training data (MOA-2) are available on request only.
- **Senotherapy trial design for fatty liver, by biomarker (perspective).** Chu et al., "Biomarker-Guided, Stage-Specific Senotherapy for Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease", [Aging and Disease, online 8 Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.14336/AD.2026.1114). A hypothesis-generating framework that sorts senescence biomarkers into selection, timing and response markers and places short senolytic clearance at the MASH and fibrosis stage, which is where the Amsterdam D+Q result sits. It proposes a design and reports no new trial data.
- **Contribution map for a small team or AI (positive path).** The highest-leverage near-term work is (1) keeping a dated, primary-source ledger of gerotherapeutic and disease-modifying RCTs like the three above, (2) building summonable explainer cards that separate established histology/safety claims from marketing, and (3) watching open methylome deposits (e.g. GEO GSE327270 for the semaglutide analysis) and ClinicalTrials.gov for replication designs. Running a MASH biopsy RCT is out of scope; synthesizing and explaining what the trials actually measured is in scope.
