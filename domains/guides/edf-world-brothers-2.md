# Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2
## Prima / Brady-Style Strategy Guide (Verse Edition)

**Platform focus:** PS4 (also applies to PS5)
**Trophy count:** 40 (1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 10 Silver, 26 Bronze)
**Estimated Platinum time:** 70–100 hours depending on weapon RNG
**Difficulty rating:** 4/10 overall (easy to moderate, main grinds are collection)

### Why this game is fun again
World Brothers 2 takes the classic EDF chaos and wraps it in charming voxel art and a team-swapping system that feels fresh. Switching between brothers and sisters mid-mission keeps every fight dynamic. The story is pure over-the-top EDF nonsense, the co-op is excellent, and the trophy list is surprisingly approachable compared to older EDF titles. Completing it feels rewarding without being soul-crushing.

### Quick-Start Controls & Basics
- Move / Aim / Shoot as standard third-person shooter
- Switch Teammates freely during missions (this is core to the game and several trophies)
- SP Moves are powerful special attacks — use them often
- Rescue fallen teammates to keep the squad strong
- Armor and Skill levels matter more than you think for later difficulties

### Trophy Roadmap (Easiest → Hardest)

#### Phase 1 – Immediate Easy Trophies (do these first)
1. **Welcome to the EDF** (Bronze)  
   Complete the tutorial. Just play it all the way through. Do not skip.

2. **Stronger Together** (Bronze)  
   Switch Teammates 20 times in a single mission.  
   Easy method: Start any early mission on Easy, spam the switch button 20+ times while fighting, then finish the mission.

3. **Moodmaker** (Bronze)  
   Use emotes a total of 10 times (only counts when you complete the mission).  
   Spam emotes during any Easy mission and clear it.

4. **Especially Special** (Silver)  
   Use SP Moves 100 times total (mission completion required).  
   This will come naturally, but you can farm it on Easy by spamming SP Moves every mission.

#### Phase 2 – Story Progress
Play through the campaign on Easy or Normal first. Story % trophies unlock as you clear missions. You do **not** need every difficulty for the % trophies in this game the same way the first World Brothers did — focus on clearing content and raising completion.

- 10% / 20% / 30% … up to **100% Story Progress** (Gold)

#### Phase 3 – Collection & Mastery
- **We Are The World** (Silver) – Recruit every Teammate  
  Check rescue points in every mission. Some characters are rare; replay missions that list the ones you’re missing.

- **Weapon Collector** (Gold) – Obtain every weapon  
  Biggest grind. Weapons have star ranks and gold variants. Higher difficulties drop better ranks. Farm short missions with multiple rescue/spawn points.

- **Armor Master** (Gold) – Reach 10,000 Armor on any Teammate  
- **Master of War** (Silver) – Reach Skill Level 8 on any Teammate

#### Phase 4 – Cleanup
- All I Need’s a Shirt (no damage mission)
- Earth Defender (10,000 enemy kills)
- Super Rescuer / Super-Duper Rescuer
- Old School Defender (solo teammate clear)
- A Hit to Remember (99,999 damage single hit)
- Perfect Weapon (5-star a weapon)

### Recommended Early Game Path
1. Finish tutorial → Welcome to the EDF
2. Play Mission 1–few on Easy while spamming teammate switches and emotes
3. Keep playing normally, always checking rescue points
4. Once you have a decent squad, start farming weapons and missing characters on higher difficulties as they unlock

### Tips for Making Progress Feel Fun
- Switch teammates constantly — it’s the soul of the game and helps multiple trophies
- Play with friends or local co-op when possible; the game shines in multiplayer
- Don’t stress the Weapon Collector early. Enjoy the story and chaos first
- Use the mission select freely. Nothing is missable
