# Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2 – Living Strategy Guide

**Pitch:** Voxel Earth shattered again. Build a team of Brothers & Sisters, switch on the fly, and fight for a cube planet that somehow still feels worth saving.

- Start with story progression and easy mission clears on lower difficulties
- Rescue every downed Brother/Sister you see (character collection is core)
- Experiment with team compositions early — switching is the real depth
- Come back for higher difficulties and specific challenges later
