## One-liner

We assemble the evidence so a UK graduate's visa application is ready before **1 January 2027**, when the grant drops from **two years to 18 months**, and a regulated adviser checks it before anyone submits.

## The clock (per writeup, sources named, not verified by Grok)

- **Immigration Rules Appendix Graduate, paragraph GR 8.1** (GOV.UK text updated 3 August 2026).
- Bachelor's and master's applicants get **2 years** if they apply **before 1 Jan 2027**, and **18 months** on or after.
- **PhDs stay at 3 years.**
- The GOV.UK Graduate visa page says the same (2 years on or before 31 Dec 2026).
- The House of Commons Library briefing (current 21 Sept 2026) records it as legislated in **Statement of Changes HC 1333**.

## Messy input

- CAS.
- An award-board result that may still say "expected."
- Attendance and UK study dates in the university system.
- Passport photo; eVisa share code.
- Proof of physical presence in the UK.
- The provider must **report successful completion to the Home Office** before the student can apply.
- Much of it sits in emails and PDFs.

## Gatekeeper

**UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI).** It won't accept an application made:
- **before the provider confirms completion**,
- **from outside the UK**, or
- **without the required UK study period**.

Which of these is refused most often: **unverified**.

## Packet

- **Case index mapped to GR 8.1:**
  - Qualification type (so PhDs are taken out of the rush).
  - Study-duration check.
  - UK-presence evidence.
  - Proof the completion report has gone in.
- **Cover sheet** stating the one date that matters: **submitted before 1 Jan 2027**.
- **"Do not file" flag** if completion hasn't been reported. **Submitting early is itself a refusal.**

## Who signs

- **The student submits.**
- Advising a named person whether to apply is **immigration advice**, which must come from an **Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) adviser** or a **solicitor or barrister**.
- **We don't advise.** We build the file **under a regulated partner** who reviews it and tells the student whether to submit. This can start from case one.
- On **23 Sept 2026** the IAA **paused competence assessments**, expected back from **November 2026** (GOV.UK news). Use advisers who are **already registered**.

## New to the company (not in other ventures yet)

- The file is finished **only if the completion report, the UK presence, and the submission timestamp all fall before 1 Jan 2027**. The same papers on 2 Jan are a different visa.
- A **cutover date** (same file, different outcome after the date), not just a due date.
- A **submission-window check**: filing too early is refused just like filing too late.

## Rescue and prevention

- **Rescue:** the December 2026 cohort racing the 1 Jan 2027 cutover.
- **Prevention:** a standing file for every cohort, kept ready whenever an immigration rule changes on a date (this generalizes to other countries' visa cutovers, **to verify**).

## Risks

- **An award not confirmed before 1 Jan can't be fixed by any pack.**
- **Doing this without a regulated adviser directing the work is unlawful.**
- New adviser licences are slow while IAA assessments are paused.
