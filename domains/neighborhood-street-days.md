## One-liner

Batch home maintenance + smart-device repair (+ optional light aging-in-place help) on the **same street / HOA cluster** so households pay less, crews waste less drive time, and older owners get reliable help before small problems force a move.

## Unit of value

**Density on one block** (target 8–12 homes on a Street Day), not total app users.

## Problem (why it exists)

- Growing solo / under-supported older adults who want to age in place; houses need maintenance + working tech, not only personal care.
- Resourceful consumers repair and keep devices; smart-home installed base has almost no organized independent local repair.
- One-off marketplace bookings are expensive, poorly routed, and feel risky for in-home strangers; manufacturers push replacement; home-care agencies are costly and care-focused.

## Model

1. Pick a street or small HOA cluster.
2. Scheduled Street Day package: seasonal exterior (gutters, pressure washing, minor repairs) + smart-home service (diagnose, battery/module, reconnect, basic training).
3. Neighbors opt in; price drops as more houses join (8–12 target).
4. One vetted crew works a tight route.
5. Optional senior add-on same day: short wellness/tech-help visit by background-checked part-timer (not licensed nursing unless separately booked clinical care).

### Revenue layers
- Take / markup on batched jobs (crews accept lower per-job for dense guaranteed work).
- Household membership ~$10–20/mo (priority, discounts, simple work log).
- Upsells: aging-in-place mods, annual smart-home contracts, referrals to licensed trades.

Start analog: Nextdoor, HOA newsletters, senior-center flyers, door hangers. Form + Stripe + shared calendar. Software only after repeatable fill rates.

## Kill list

- Fill rate collapses → discount evaporates
- Insurance / background-check cost vs take rate
- Weather / seasonality on exterior
- Crossing into unlicensed home health
- Competing as generic gig app instead of street density
